# Rising Lion's districts (1.21). Each of our special buildings sits in a district of its own, as vanilla's do:
# - the temples of the old gods and the Temple in Jerusalem in a province's great project district, which every province
#   has; their great projects place them with set_great_district (common/great_projects/types/)
# - Masada, the cleared Temple Mount and the holy site buildings of Mesopotamianism and Arameanism in a special district
#   slot (province history, add_holy_site_fallback_special_district_slot_effect, the Third Temple effects)
# Names: <district>: "$building_type_<building>$", like vanilla's (localization/english/jip_buildings_l_english.yml)
