# Rising Lion — Canaanite Flavor Pack: Mod Analysis

**Version:** 0.14.0  
**Game:** Crusader Kings 3 (supported version 1.20.*)  
**Workshop ID:** 3501948259  
**Tags:** Religion, Culture, Decisions, Flavor

---

## Overview

Rising Lion is a CK3 mod focused on bringing deep historical and cultural flavor to the Levant, Near East, and surrounding regions. It centers on Jewish/Israelite heritage and the ancient civilizations of the area, adding new cultures, faiths, playable dynasties, decisions, buildings, and government types.

Generator scripts (`make_*.py`, `add_event_source_badge.py`) are in `tools/`; they use absolute paths to the mod and the vanilla game folder.

Plans for the next versions are in `.docs/` (the only tracked files there; the rest of `.docs` is gitignored): `v0.15_beth_aramaye.md` (Arameans, Assyrians, Sabians, Mandaeans) and `v0.16_exilarchs_and_maccabees.md` (the Jewish features). Add new plans to the matching version file.

## Cultures

New or expanded cultures, each with their own heritage, innovations, and traditions:
- Hebrew, Edomite, Canaanite, Phoenician, Radhanite, Bavlim, Aramean, Copt
- **Nabati** (`nabati`, `common/culture/cultures/01_nabati.txt`): the Aramaic peasants of the Sawad (heritage Syriac, ethos Communal; Agrarian, Philosopher Culture, Language Scholars, Parochialism; `name_list_nabati`). At 867 Wasit, Tib, the Marshes, Badaraya, Iskaf and Ubulla. Place names: the Aramean ones
- **Aramaic place names**: the Aramean (`syriac`, `name_list_aramean`, character names copied from vanilla's `name_list_assyrian`), Assyrian (`name_list_assyrian`) and Nabati lists' cultural names are written into `jip_landed_titles.txt` by `tools/make_aramaic_place_names.py` from its table (Aramean: Iron-Age Aramaic, else Classical Syriac; Assyrian: Neo-Assyrian, else Classical Syriac; Nabati: the Aramean name). Change the table and rerun it; don't edit those three lists in the titles file by hand

Players can reform or restore cultures through major decisions.

Jewish cultures copy the innovation history of their neighbours (`history/cultures/<culture>.txt`, generated by `make_jewish_culture_history.py` from vanilla's files; rerun after vanilla updates): Ashkenazi ← heritage_central_germanic, Sephardi ← heritage_iberian, Bavlim/Radhanite/Hebrew ← levantine (Mashriqi), Kochinim ← heritage_dravidian, Edomite ← heritage_arabic (plus war camels from 1.1.1); the generator also gives Nabati ← levantine. A culture's own file overrides its heritage file.

## Religions & Faiths

Judaism is the central religious focus, with two distinct faiths:

- **Rabbinism** — Decentralized, no head of faith. Holy sites: Jerusalem, Safed, Sinai, Alexandria, Babylon. Doctrines: communal identity, legalism, religious legal pronouncements.
- **Karaism** — A rival Jewish faith with its own Exilarchate line.
- **Mandaeans and Sabians** — vanilla's `mandaean_faith` (rites `mandeaism` and `sabianism`), redefined in `common/religion/faith_types/jip_gnostic_faith_types.txt` (keep in sync with vanilla's block): head of faith the Rish Amma (`d_rish_amma`, landless like vanilla's heads of faith; `jip_rish_amma_setup_effect` creates a holder at game start), holy sites Tib and the Marshes (eminent), Harran, Basra, Shushtar. At 867 Harran is Sabian, Tib and the Marshes Mandaean
- **Kohanism** — Introduces the Kohen Gadol (High Priest) title with its own flavorization and council position.

The Jewish faiths' rites are in `common/religion/rite_types/jip_jewish_rite_types.txt`. Vanilla also defines rites for Karaism, Haymanot, Malabarism, Samaritanism and Kabarism in `02_mainline_rite_types.txt`, so the mod replaces that file by path with a copy without them (generated by `make_mainline_rite_types_override.py`; rerun after every vanilla update, and if vanilla renames the file, delete the old copy: until 1.20.1 it was `03_`, and the stale copy defined every rite twice)

## Government Type: Mishpacha (mishpacha_government)

A custom government for landless Jewish families, modeled on the historical Nasi. Key rules:
- Landless playable; families hold a duchy-tier noble family title (`d_jip_*` in `common/landed_titles/jip_landless_titles.txt`) and an estate
- City holdings only; a family that gains land adopts its liege's government (or feudal)
- `mechanic_type = mishpacha`, not administrative: landless heads fail vanilla's `is_landed_or_landless_administrative_or_theocratic`, so most vanilla court positions, events and admin perks are closed to them. Gate Mishpacha content with `government_has_flag = government_is_mishpacha`
- No council and no influence (the influence flag is off); legitimacy and noble families are on
- Dearer MaA upkeep (no levy, knight, MaA regiment or size penalty), cheaper mercenaries, doubled diplomatic range, +15% income, +10% learning and stewardship XP, +10 travel safety, +5 courtier and guest opinion
- Primary heritage: Israelite
- Realm CoA: the engine picks the shape from the government's name, `gfx/interface/icons/realm_frames/mishpacha_government_{28,44,62,86,115}_{frame,shadow}.dds` and `_mask.dds` (a banner with a Torah-mantle hem: two scallops meeting in a point; generated by `make_mishpacha_coa.py` from a Gemini black-on-white silhouette, with vanilla's cloth texture, shading and shadow; the top must stay flat for the tier bar). Above it sits a kippah (`gfx/interface/coat_of_arms/jip_mishpacha_topframe_<size>.dds`), or for an Exilarch of either line the Keter Shem Tov (`jip_exilarch_topframe_<size>.dds`, checked by the `jip_coa_is_exilarch` scripted GUI); both are made from Gemini images on chroma green by `make_mishpacha_crown.py <image> <preview> <max_w> <max_h> <name>` (kippah 80x48, Keter 96x70). `gui/shared/coat_of_arms.gui` is a full copy of the file (generated by `make_coa_gui_override.py`; rerun after every vanilla or ARB update), built on Aligned Realm Banners' copy when that mod is installed (see Compatibility), that hides the tier crown for Mishpacha heads and adds the kippah or Keter to the realm CoAs; the tier bar stays, as for adventurers. The bookmark screen uses the bookmark entry's `government` (all Mishpacha bookmark characters set `mishpacha_government`) for the shape and decoration; a bookmark character isn't a real character, so the Exilarchs are matched by bookmark name (`EXILARCH_BOOKMARKS` in the generator: Hasdai II and Boaz I in 867; add new ones there). Save games always show the kippah

### Realm and migration
- Families are vassals of the realm their estate lies in, under the "Charter of Privileges" contract (communal taxes, religious rights, Beit Din and trade rights); Muslim lieges may levy jizya
- Moving the estate to another realm switches liege; AI families migrate to more welcoming realms on their own
- The engine only sets a travelling estate down where `can_move_domicile` (`common/scripted_rules/jip_scripted_rules.txt`) allows it, a city for Mishpacha, so a trip ending elsewhere used to leave it in the old realm. On arrival `jewish_admin_migration_on_action` first runs `jip_mishpacha_settle_estate_on_arrival_effect` (the city reached, else the realm's nearest city), then `jip_mishpacha_join_estate_realm_effect`. Mishpacha families skip the rule's war check; AI migration still waits out wars
- `jip_mishpacha_change_liege_effect` (`01_jip_liege_setup_effects.txt`) makes the family a vassal of a king or emperor, but a tributary (`tributary_settled`) of a duke or lesser ruler, since a duchy-tier family title can't be a duke's vassal (ranks are compared with `tier_duchy`, not the head's `highest_held_title_tier`). It runs at game start for independent families (`jip_liege_effect_setup`) and on estate arrival in a new realm (`jewish_admin_migration_on_action`), which ends the old liege or suzerain tie
- Vanilla's `yearly_playable_pulse` destroys the family titles of independent rulers who hold nothing else, which tributaries are too. `common/on_action/yearly_on_actions.txt` is a full copy of vanilla's file (an on_action's effect can't be appended to) with one `NOT = { government_has_flag = government_is_mishpacha }` added; it's generated by `make_yearly_on_actions_override.py`, so rerun that after every vanilla update
- A head who leaves Judaism steps down for the nearest Jewish man of the house; Mishpacha Succession covers family titles and Exilarchs
- Game start (`jip_jewish_families_game_start_on_action` in on_game_start, before the lobby, Roads to Power only): `jip_spawn_jewish_families_effect` creates up to 60 new families from per-region quotas (`common/scripted_effects/01_jip_jewish_family_spawn_effects.txt`), one per county, in cities of Christian, Muslim or Jewish realms that haven't expelled the Jews (`jip_jewish_family_home_county_trigger`), weighted by development, royal seats and Jewish counties. Each head (`jip_jewish_family_head_template`, aged 20–40, new dynasty) gets the family title and estate the same way as a dispossessed lord, the local ruler as liege or suzerain, and vanilla's `game_rule_create_spouse_and_children`. Culture follows the region, faith is mostly Rabbinic (some Merkabah; Karaites in Egypt, the Caliphate, Byzantium and the Khazar steppe). Root is empty in on_game_start, so nothing in this chain may use root

### Titles
- Family heads are **Mar/Geveret** and their spouses Geveret/Mar, whatever the liege (`top_liege = no`, priority 302); estates are "the X Household"
- Family titles use the `duchy_mishpacha` tier name ("Mishpacha"); without it the landless titles log "Failed to find any valid flavorization"
- Exilarch and Kohen Gadol (priority 310) outrank Mar
- Title flavorization `flag` checks did not work for a Davidic "Nasi" variant; it was dropped

### Estate buildings (`common/domiciles/buildings/jip_mishpacha_estate_buildings.txt`)
- New branches with custom icons: **Yeshiva** (library), **Beit Din** (office), **Beit Knesset** (temple; slows migration, discourages expulsion)
- **Counting House** replaces the market line; **Hachnasat Orchim** replaces the Guest Room (piety instead of influence); both keep vanilla names/icons
- Influence-free copies replace vanilla Temple/Crypt/Prison/Trophy Room/Silk/Office lines; the vanilla versions are hidden in `jip_vanilla_estate_overrides.txt`, and existing estates are swapped by `jip_mishpacha_replace_estate_buildings_effect`
- The Villa's influence becomes prestige (`jip_mishpacha_villa_1..5` modifiers)

### Court positions (`common/court_positions/types/jip_jewish_court_positions.txt`, generated)
- Mishpacha heads can appoint all eight: **Rosh Yeshiva, Av Beit Din, Paytan, Chazan, Gabbai Tzedakah, Shaliach, Sofer, Parnas**
- Landed Jewish rulers (duke and above) only get **Av Beit Din** and **Shaliach**, which no vanilla position covers for them; the rest duplicate vanilla positions they already have
- Holders must be adult Jews; the Yeshiva, Beit Din, Beit Knesset and Counting House raise the matching position's aptitude, shown as building parameters
- `minimum_rank` is a hard engine gate: positions above county also check `highest_held_title_tier` in `valid_position`, or appointments silently fail
- Tasks: positions with a vanilla counterpart share its tasks through `common/court_positions/tasks/jip_vanilla_task_overrides.txt` (exact vanilla copies with our positions added to `court_position_types` and the vanilla position's DLC added to `is_shown`; keep in sync with vanilla). New tasks are in `jip_jewish_court_position_tasks.txt`. Both files are generated
- Vanilla tasks that look up their own position by name (Educate Children, Organize Court) can't be shared: they would show but do nothing
- All eight and the Court Financier are full-time jobs (`jip_jewish_court_position_validity_trigger`): one per character, and not alongside a vanilla full-time position. Vanilla positions don't check ours, so a landed ruler's Av Beit Din or Shaliach could still take a vanilla job
- At game start (`jip_mishpacha_game_start_courtiers_on_action`) `jip_mishpacha_generate_courtiers_effect` adds a scholar, steward and envoy when no adult Jew at court has 10+ in that skill, and a cantor if the court still has fewer than four adult Jews (templates in `common/scripted_character_templates/jip_jewish_courtier_templates.txt`)
- The Sofer combines the Seneschal and Chronicler (stewardship and diplomacy aptitude; the Chronicler's legend tasks plus Keep the Estate's Affairs)

### Contracts (`common/task_contracts/jip_mishpacha_contracts.txt`)
- Vanilla task contracts aren't tied to adventurers (admin and mandala rulers own them too), so Mishpacha heads own real contracts; only generation is ours. `jip_mishpacha_refresh_contracts_effect` calls `populate_task_contracts_for_area` on the estate with `jip_mishpacha_contracts_group`: yearly, on estate arrival, on a new head, at game start
- Player heads with Roads to Power only (`jip_mishpacha_contract_owner_trigger`); AI heads get a hidden yearly roll (`jip_mishpacha_ai_contract_effect`)
- Offers expire through the `jip_contract_fresh` variable (395 days) in `valid_to_keep`; `use_diplomatic_range = yes`, `travel = no`
- Nine non-martial contracts. `make_contract_events.py` generates their events (`events/jip_mishpacha_contract_events.txt`), the contract scheme types (`common/schemes/scheme_types/jip_mishpacha_contract_schemes.txt`), the schemes' approach modifiers (`common/modifiers/jip_contract_scheme_modifiers.txt`) and the event and scheme localization
- **Scheme contracts** (Caravan, Envoy, Ransom, Translate, News), like vanilla's laamp contract schemes: `.00X0` fires on acceptance and each approach starts a scheme (one type per contract and skill, `jip_contract_<contract>_<skill>_scheme`), sets its agent slots (success, balanced or speed mix) and adds approach modifiers (odds, pace). Phases and HUD clicks go through vanilla's preparations window (`scheme_critical_moments.0002`, via `jip_contract_scheme_prep_effect`), which executes into the hidden `.00X2`: it rolls the scheme's success chance (`jip_contract_roll_scheme_effect`) and fires `.00X3` (`window = scheme_successful_event`) or `.00X4` (`window = scheme_failed_event`), whose option runs `jip_contract_finish_effect` and ends the scheme. Estate and court bonuses are scripted modifiers in `common/scripted_modifiers/jip_contract_scheme_modifiers.txt`. A scheme that becomes invalid invalidates its contract, and an invalidated contract ends its scheme
- **Event contracts** (Loan, Tolls, Physician, Responsum): `.00X0` fires 2–4 months after acceptance: each option is an approach calling `jip_contract_choose_approach_effect = { SKILL BONUS APPROACH EVENT }`, which rolls the outcome (critical 30% / success 70% of the chance, failure the rest; chances are `jip_contract_<skill>_chance_value`, raised by the matching building and court position) and stores it with the approach on the contract. `.00X1` fires a few days later with text for that approach and outcome; its option runs `jip_contract_finish_effect`, i.e. the approach's own trade-off (`jip_contract_<contract>_approach_effect`) and then `complete_task_contract`
- In `task_contract_reward` effects `this` is the contract but `root` is whoever completed it (the event's character): reach the contract through `scope:task_contract`, never `root`
- One active contract, two with a Sofer; a Shaliach adds an offer per refresh. Loan and caravan stakes are paid on acceptance and refunded on invalidation

Title flavorizations also include: Emperor, King, Duke (Israelite heritage), Exilarch, Kohen Gadol, Kohen (chaplain position).

## Jews in Gentile Realms

- **Expel the Jews / Readmit the Jews** decisions for Christian and Muslim rulers, with AI weighting from traits and tenets; expelled lords may resist in one shared war and become Mishpacha families if they lose
- **Court Financier** court position for non-Jewish dukes and above: domain taxes and Jewish opinion, angers zealots, debt-collection events
- **Shtadlanut** (`common/character_interactions/jip_shtadlan_interactions.txt`, generated by `make_shtadlan_interactions.py`): four requests a landless Mishpacha head puts to a ruler in diplomatic range: Readmit the Jews (lifts the edict despite its cooldown), Letter of Protection (`jip_jews_protected` on the primary title for 15 years; blocks Expel the Jews, raises the migration host score), Release a Prisoner (kin first) and Ease the Charter (liege only: waive jizya, else lower communal taxes). Gold, prestige, hook and offer-a-favour send options (the last gives the ruler a `favor_hook` on the family, only if they hold no hook on it) are paid only on acceptance. AI acceptance is `jip_shtadlan_ai_accept_modifier`: request difficulty (`jip_shtadlan_<request>_difficulty_value`), minus `jip_jewish_hostility_value` (the Expel the Jews weights), half the ruler's opinion, faith hostility to Judaism, rank (`jip_shtadlan_rank_value`), not being the family's liege or suzerain, a favour granted in the last 10 years (`jip_shtadlan_granted` on the ruler), a Jewish Court Financier, a petitioning Parnas, and the sweeteners (a strong hook guarantees it). The AI never sends them

## The Exilarchate

A central political mechanic of the mod. Two rival figurehead titles:
- **d_exilarchate** — Rabbinic Exilarchate, held by the Davidite/Bostanite/Nathanite lines
- **d_karaite_exilarchate** — Karaite Exilarchate, held by the Ananite line

Both use **Exilarchate Elective** succession: dynasty members and claimants of the same faith vote. Players can use the **Claim Exilarchate** decision to start a scheme, gain a claim, become an elector, and campaign for the title. The Exilarchate has deep history files going back to -596.1.1 (the Babylonian exile).

## The Davidic Line Trait

**Type:** Fame trait, inheritable  
**Inheritance:** Male line only, 100% chance, does not inherit from real father (illegitimate children excluded)  
**Shown in ruler designer:** No — bloodline only

### Stat Effects
- +5 zealot opinion
- +10 same-faith opinion
- +0.2 monthly prestige
- Initial legitimacy boost flag

### Interaction with the Exilarchate
In Exilarchate elections, candidates with `davidic_line` receive a bonus to elector votes, scaled by each elector's zeal. Devout electors weight Davidic descent heavily, making the trait a major political asset when campaigning for the Exilarch title.

### Davidic Line Legacy Track
Unlocked when the dynasty dynast carries the `davidic_line` trait (or has already purchased into the track). Five perks, thematically mirroring David's legacy:

| Perk | Name | Effects |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | Traditional Slingers | Skirmisher MaA: +2 size, +20 damage, +20 pursuit, toughness/screen multipliers |
| 2 | David's Virility | +1 seduction scheme slot, +25% fertility, +5 years of fertility |
| 3 | Shepherd Kings | +2 Diplomacy, +0.5 monthly piety, +10 vassal opinion |
| 4 | Solomon's Wisdom | +3 Learning, +1 language-learning scheme slot |
| 5 | Clever Administrators | +50% development growth, +1 domain limit, +20% tax income |

## Major Decisions

### Culture Decisions
- **Form Hebrew Culture** — Requires Israelite heritage, Jewish faith, control of Palestine/Transjordan, Jerusalem of same faith
- **Form Edomite Culture** — Requires Israelite/Phoenician heritage, capital in Edom region
- **Form Canaanite Culture** — Requires Phoenician heritage or Israelite capital in Phoenicia region

### Title Formation Decisions
- Kingdom of Judea, Kingdom of Edom, Kingdoms of Upper/Lower Egypt
- Phoenicia (Tyre, Sidon, Byblos)
- **Empire of Canaan** — De jure empire of the Levant
- **Empire of Kemet** — Egypt, Nubia, Blemmyia
- **Hegemony of the Fertile Crescent** — Large Near Eastern hegemony
- **Beth Nahrain** — Available to Israelites and Canaanites

### Jewish-Specific Decisions
- **Claim Exilarchate** — Start a scheme to gain a claim and become an elector
- **Reestablish Canaanite City** — Create a Phoenician trade republic vassal in a historic city (Tyre, Sidon, Beirut, Tripoli, Famagusta, Nicosia, Latakia)

## Unique Buildings

- **Masada** (3 tiers) — Special building at b_zughar, capital of c_negev. Tiers: Ruins of Old Masada (150g, +200 garrison, +1 fort) → Restored Masadan Fortifications (400g, +500 garrison, +2 fort, +5% levy) → Grand Fortifications of Masada (1000g, +1000 garrison, +4 fort, +10% levy, +5 defender bonus)

## Mod branding (DLC-style icon and event banner)

**When creating events for this mod, always add the DLC banner** to every event that opens a window, right after its `theme` line:
```
	widgets = {
		widget = {
			gui = "jip_event_source_badge"
			container = "foreground_shader_vfx_container"
		}
	}
```
(Skip hidden events and events with `window = scheme_*_event`. Generators that write events must emit it.)

A mod can't be a DLC or an event's `content_source`, so:
- **Icon** `gfx/interface/icons/dlc/jip_rising_lion.dds` (120x120): a Gemini painting in vanilla's flavour-pack frame (Northern Lords, Fate of Iberia, Legacy of Persia share it), made by `make_dlc_icon.py <painting> <x0> <y0> <size>`; used as the texticon `@rising_lion_icon!`. It is deliberately not in the DLC rows of the main menu, bookmark screen or lobby: reaching them means copying vanilla GUI (all of `frontend_main.gui`, or the `button_show_dlc_list` type) that would need updating with every game patch
- **Event banner** `gfx/interface/illustrations/jip_event_source_banner.dds` (526x128, shown at 263x64): a Gemini painting in place of the art of vanilla's `dlc_009` event decoration, keeping its fade, ledge and shadow (`make_dlc_banner.py <painting> <y0>`). Every visible event adds `widgets = { widget = { gui = "jip_event_source_badge" container = "foreground_shader_vfx_container" } }` (`gui/event_window_widgets/jip_event_source_badge.gui`); new events need it too (`add_event_source_badge.py` adds it to all of them; `make_contract_events.py` emits it). The scheme outcome windows (`window = scheme_*_event`) and letter events have no such container

## Compatibility

- Compatible with **Cultures Expanded** mod (v0.10.1+)
- Compatible with **Aligned Realm Banners** (workshop 3823433423), which replaces the same `gui/shared/coat_of_arms.gui`: ours is built on its copy, so with both enabled Rising Lion must load after it and keeps ARB's overlay alignment of top decorations and its march/palatinate/castellan decorations. The file must work without ARB too, so its references are guarded: ARB's scripted GUIs are replaced by our copies (`common/scripted_guis/jip_arb_compat_scripted_guis.txt`), shown only when ARB's localization `setting_arb_contract_decor_on` resolves, and ARB's textures are chosen with `Select_CString` with a vanilla fallback. ARB's own game rule can't be read (it doesn't exist without ARB), so its decorations show whatever that rule is set to
- Integrates with the base game's **Persian Struggle** (Rabbinism and Karaism listed as involved faiths)
- Integrates with base game tributary setup system
