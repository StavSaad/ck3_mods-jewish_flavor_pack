# Rising Lion — Canaanite Flavor Pack: Mod Analysis

**Version:** 0.14.0  
**Game:** Crusader Kings 3 (supported version 1.20.*)  
**Workshop ID:** 3501948259  
**Tags:** Religion, Culture, Decisions, Flavor

---

## Overview

Rising Lion is a CK3 mod focused on bringing deep historical and cultural flavor to the Levant, Near East, and surrounding regions. It centers on Jewish/Israelite heritage and the ancient civilizations of the area, adding new cultures, faiths, playable dynasties, decisions, buildings, and government types.

Generator scripts (`make_*.py`, `add_event_source_badge.py`) are in `tools/`; they use absolute paths to the mod and the vanilla game folder.

Plans for the next versions are in `.docs/` (the only tracked files there; the rest of `.docs` is gitignored): `v0.15_beth_aramaye.md` (Arameans, Assyrians, Sabians, Mandaeans) and `v0.16_exilarchs_and_maccabees.md` (the Jewish features). Add new plans to the matching version file.

## Cultures

New or expanded cultures, each with their own heritage, innovations, and traditions:
- Hebrew, Edomite, Canaanite, Phoenician, Radhanite, Bavlim, Aramean, Copt
- **Nabati** (`nabati`, `common/culture/cultures/01_nabati.txt`): the Aramaic peasants of the Sawad (heritage Syriac, ethos Communal; Agrarian, Philosopher Culture, Language Scholars, Parochialism; `name_list_nabati`). At 867 Wasit, Tib, the Marshes, Badaraya, Iskaf and Ubulla. Place names: the Aramean ones
- **Aramaic place names**: the Aramean (`syriac`, `name_list_aramean`, character names copied from vanilla's `name_list_assyrian`), Assyrian (`name_list_assyrian`) and Nabati lists' cultural names are written into `jip_landed_titles.txt` by `tools/make_aramaic_place_names.py` from its table (Aramean: Iron-Age Aramaic, else Classical Syriac; Assyrian: Neo-Assyrian, else Classical Syriac; Nabati: the Aramean name). Change the table and rerun it; don't edit those three lists in the titles file by hand
- **Cultural men-at-arms** (`common/men_at_arms_types/jip_cultural_maa_types.txt`, costs in `common/script_values/jip_men_at_arms_values.txt`), each unlocked like vanilla's by a parameter of a tradition (`common/culture/traditions/jip_maa_traditions.txt`) open to one heritage: Palmyrene Archers (Aramean) and Clibanarii (Assyrian), Syriac; Bowmen of Seir (Edomite, in place of Desert Ribat), Israelite; Sacred Band (Phoenician, Carthaginian), Phoenician; vanilla's Nile Archers (Kemi, Ancient Egyptian) through Archers of the Two Lands, Egyptian. Add new MaA traditions to the Hebrew formation's `handle_maa_traditions_for_culture` list (`01_jip_decisions_effects.txt`). The `@` values in a MaA file are local to it, so vanilla's are repeated there

Players can reform or restore cultures through major decisions.

Jewish cultures copy the innovation history of their neighbours (`history/cultures/<culture>.txt`, generated by `make_jewish_culture_history.py` from vanilla's files; rerun after vanilla updates): Ashkenazi ← heritage_central_germanic, Sephardi ← heritage_iberian, Bavlim/Radhanite/Hebrew ← levantine (Mashriqi), Kochinim ← heritage_dravidian, Edomite ← heritage_arabic (plus war camels from 1.1.1); the generator also gives Nabati ← levantine. A culture's own file overrides its heritage file.

## Religions & Faiths

Judaism is the central religious focus, with two distinct faiths:

- **Rabbinism** — Decentralized, no head of faith. Holy sites: Jerusalem, Safed, Sinai, Alexandria, Babylon. Doctrines: communal identity, legalism, religious legal pronouncements.
- **Karaism** — A rival Jewish faith with its own Exilarchate line.
- **Mandaeans and Sabians** — vanilla's `mandaean_faith` (rites `mandeaism` and `sabianism`), redefined in `common/religion/faith_types/jip_gnostic_faith_types.txt` (keep in sync with vanilla's block): head of faith the Rish Amma (`d_rish_amma`, landless like vanilla's heads of faith; `jip_rish_amma_setup_effect` creates a holder at game start), holy sites Tib and the Marshes (eminent), Harran, Basra, Shushtar. Icon: the drabsha (`mandaean`), also on the Mandaean rite, which `common/religion/rite_types/jip_gnostic_rite_types.txt` redefines for it (a copy of vanilla's `mandeaism`; keep in sync). At 867 Harran is Sabian, Tib and the Marshes Mandaean. The Rish Amma's CoA (`jip_titles_coa.txt`) carries the drabsha as a colored emblem, `ce_jip_drabsha`, built by `tools/make_coa_emblem.py <drawing> <name>` from a Gemini drawing in flat code colours (white = color1, red = color2, green = color3, black lines = shading; vanilla's emblems keep color2 in the red channel, color3 in the green, shading in the blue) and listed for the CoA designer in `gfx/coat_of_arms/colored_emblems/jip_coa_emblems.txt`
- **Mesopotamianism** (`mesopotamian_pagan`) — reformed pagan faith in the mod's `canaanite_religion` (shown as "Levantine", with Canaanism), in `jip_canaanite_faith_types.txt`. Rites `ashurism`, Ashurism (main: Ashur, Vicegerent of Ashur tenet, Warmonger, Ritual Celebrations) and `sinism` (Sin of Harran: Astrology, Esotericism, Sanctity of Nature, its own gods in the rite's localization). Holy sites Harran and Nineveh (eminent), Arbela, Manbij, Damascus, Palmyra. Tenet icons (Kehunah, Vicegerent of Ashur) are built by `tools/make_tenet_icon.py <cut.png> <tenet_key>` from a cut-out Gemini object over vanilla-style light strokes. Faith and rite icons (100x100) are built by `tools/make_faith_icon.py <image> <icon_name> [--reformed]` from a Gemini emblem on flat #00FF00 (or any flat colour, read from the corners); `--reformed` also writes `<icon_name>_reformed` with vanilla's glow. `--palette` recolours a silver emblem; `tools/make_tanit_icons.py` builds all the Tanit icons that way (Canaanism gold, Punism silver, `canaanite_01`..`06` for custom faiths). `--inlay` recolours only a blue inlay; `tools/make_mesopotamian_icons.py` builds the winged disc's custom-faith colours (`mesopotamian_01`..`06`) that way, and `tools/make_harranian_icons.py` the crescent standard's (`harranian_01`..`06`, `--accent warm` for its gold disc and tassel caps). No one holds it at any start; the Sabians restore it through the great project `jip_gp_temple_of_sin` (`common/great_projects/types/jip_great_projects.txt`, modelled on vanilla's `pam_cathedral_01`; the system needs no DLC): an independent Sabian ruler whose realm holds Harran rebuilds the Ehulhul, the great building `jip_temple_of_sin_01` (works only for Sabians and the old faith; on the map it borrows the Tomb of Cyrus's model and icon, vanilla having no ziggurat), which unlocks conversion and fires `jip_sabian.0001`; its icon and illustration are built by `tools/make_great_project_art.py <name> --icon <image> --illustration <image> [--y 0-1]` from Gemini art (the icon a painted miniature on flat green), where the builder may proclaim the old gods (`jip_proclaim_old_gods_effect`: ruler, capital and Harran; Assyrian and Nabati take Ashurism, others Sinism). A faith with no followers counts as not founded and is hidden from the faith list unless its history sets `created = yes` (`history/faiths/jip_faiths.txt`, as vanilla does for Karaism)
- **Festivals** — feast variants, like vanilla's Tsagaan Sar: `common/activities/activity_types/feast.txt` is a full copy of vanilla's, generated by `tools/make_feast_override.py` (rerun after every vanilla update; add variants to its `VARIANTS` table, as 0.16's other Jewish feasts will). Akitu (Mesopotamian pagans, March-April), Feast of Nisan (rites `sabianism`, `sinism`, March-April), Parwanaya and Dehwa Rabba (rite `mandeaism`, months computed from the year by `common/script_values/jip_feast_values.txt`: the Mandaean calendar has no leap days). Pesach (`jip_feast_type_pesach`, every Jewish faith, March-April; the first of 0.16's Jewish feasts, built early): the seder chain varies by faith (`jip_pesach_haggadah_trigger`: the Haggadah, or the Karaites', Samaritans' and Beta Israel's Torah-only seder, 5006), with more for an Exilarch, a host who holds Jerusalem and a rebuilt Temple; Jewish playable characters who sat at no grand seder get a household seder each April (`jip_household_seder_on_action`, `jip_feast.5020`). Every variant's `is_shown` checks its faith and its month, so it is hidden out of season; `is_valid` holds only its cooldown. The override's meal and toast phases call `jip_feast_meal_phase_effect` and `jip_feast_toast_phase_effect` (`01_jip_feast_effects.txt`), which start each variant's events (`events/jip_feast_events.txt`, triggers `jip_is_<variant>`); the year's omen is `var:jip_feast_omen` on the host. Mesopotamian dukes and above who skip Akitu for 8 years get `jip_akitu_neglected_1` (12: `_2`); for Sinism the Feast of Nisan counts too (`jip_akitu_last_year`, reset in `jip_feast.1004` and `.2004`). The override also lets Mishpacha heads host feasts (`jip_can_host_feast_trigger`). It also gives each variant a background scene (`BACKGROUNDS`; vanilla's halls follow the location's building graphics and miss ours). The planning window's art is found by the option key (`illustrations/activity_types/<variant>.dds` 460x1100, `icons/activity_types/<variant>_icon.dds` 78x78 black glyph), built by `tools/make_feast_type_art.py <variant> --painting <image> [--x 0-1] --icon <silhouette>` from Gemini art
- **Sabian Court Astrologer** (`jip_sabian_astrologer_court_position`, in `jip_court_positions.txt` beside the Court Financier; vanilla's Stargazer is nomads only): employed by settled dukes and above who are Muslim, Sabian or Mesopotamian pagan, or rule from the Middle East or Anatolia, not Mandaeans, and by Sabian Familial heads, the only Familial heads who can (`jip_can_employ_sabian_astrologer_trigger`; the recruit decision is shown to them too); held by an adult Sabian or Mesopotamian pagan, full time. Its tasks are ours (`common/court_positions/tasks/jip_sabian_astrologer_tasks.txt`, by hand: the Stargazer's are for the steppe). Horoscopes in `events/jip_astrologer_events.txt` from `jip_astrologer_on_actions.txt`: before a war (`on_war_started`), at a birth (the child's ruling planet for life, `jip_star_<planet>`), at a marriage, and a Muslim zealot's objection (yearly); readings succeed with `jip_astrologer_reading_chance_value`. Players find one through vanilla's Recruit for a Court Position decision, overridden by key (only that decision) in `common/decisions/jip_recruit_court_position_decision.txt`, generated by `tools/make_recruit_decision_override.py` (rerun after every vanilla update): its option sends to Harran for two Sabian candidates (`jip_astrologer.0010`). That decision is player-only, so AI rulers hire one through a hidden yearly roll (`jip_astrologer_ai_recruit_on_action`)
- **Kohanism** — Introduces the Kohen Gadol (High Priest) title with its own flavorization and council position.

Locked faiths: Kohanism, Canaanism (both rites) and Mesopotamianism (both rites) can't be converted to until an event restores them: their rite types have `convert = no`, lifted by `set_can_convert_to_rite = yes`. Kohanism opens when the Third Temple is built (`jip_unlock_kohanism_conversion_effect` in `jip_third_temple_on_action`), Canaanism with the Phoenicia decision or vanilla's Restore Carthage (gaining `k_carthage`; `jip_unlock_canaanite_conversion_effect`, `jip_carthage_restored_on_action`). Mesopotamianism with the Temple of Sin (`jip_unlock_mesopotamian_conversion_effect`)

The Jewish faiths' rites are in `common/religion/rite_types/jip_jewish_rite_types.txt`. Vanilla also defines rites for Karaism, Haymanot, Malabarism, Samaritanism and Kabarism in `02_mainline_rite_types.txt`, so the mod replaces that file by path with a copy without them (generated by `make_mainline_rite_types_override.py`; rerun after every vanilla update, and if vanilla renames the file, delete the old copy: until 1.20.1 it was `03_`, and the stale copy defined every rite twice)

## Government Type: Familial (familial_government); Mishpacha for Jews

A custom government for landless families of protected minorities, modeled on the historical Nasi (`common/governments/jip_familial_government_types.txt`). One government for every faith: Jewish families call it Mishpacha (the `mishpacha` game concept is an alias of `familial`), and what is Jewish about it (Mar/Geveret, the kippah, the Jewish court positions, the Yeshiva, Beit Din and Beit Knesset, contracts, Shtadlanut, AI migration, expulsion) checks `jip_is_jewish_family_trigger` (the flag and the Judaism religion); the shared mechanics check the `government_is_familial` flag. Internal names keep `mishpacha` (`jip_mishpacha_*` effects and triggers, `mishpacha_vassal`, `mishpacha_succession_law`). Saves from before the rename (0.14) are incompatible. Key rules:
- Landless playable; families hold a duchy-tier noble family title (`d_jip_*` in `common/landed_titles/jip_landless_titles.txt`) and an estate
- City holdings only; a family that gains land adopts its liege's government (or feudal)
- `mechanic_type = familial`, not administrative: landless heads fail vanilla's `is_landed_or_landless_administrative_or_theocratic`, so most vanilla court positions, events and admin perks are closed to them. Gate Familial content with `government_has_flag = government_is_familial`, Jewish family content with `jip_is_jewish_family_trigger`
- No council and no influence (the influence flag is off); legitimacy and noble families are on
- Dearer MaA upkeep (no levy, knight, MaA regiment or size penalty), cheaper mercenaries, doubled diplomatic range, +15% income, +10% learning and stewardship XP, +10 travel safety, +5 courtier and guest opinion
- Primary heritage: Israelite
- Realm CoA: the engine picks the shape from the government's name, `gfx/interface/icons/realm_frames/familial_government_{28,44,62,86,115}_{frame,shadow}.dds` and `_mask.dds` (a banner with a Torah-mantle hem: two scallops meeting in a point; generated by `make_mishpacha_coa.py` from a Gemini black-on-white silhouette, with vanilla's cloth texture, shading and shadow; the top must stay flat for the tier bar). Above it sits, for Jewish heads (the `jip_coa_is_jewish` scripted GUI), a kippah (`gfx/interface/coat_of_arms/jip_mishpacha_topframe_<size>.dds`), or for an Exilarch of either line the Keter Shem Tov (`jip_exilarch_topframe_<size>.dds`, checked by the `jip_coa_is_exilarch` scripted GUI); both are made from Gemini images on a flat chroma colour (green; blue for green objects) by `make_mishpacha_crown.py <image> <preview> <max_w> <max_h> <name> [--no-flatten]` (kippah 80x48, Keter 96x70; `--no-flatten` keeps a shape with no rim, like the klila, from being bent straight). `gui/shared/coat_of_arms.gui` is a full copy of the file (generated by `make_coa_gui_override.py`; rerun after every vanilla or ARB update), built on Aligned Realm Banners' copy when that mod is installed (see Compatibility), that hides the tier crown for Familial heads and adds the kippah or Keter to the realm CoAs; the tier bar stays, as for adventurers. The bookmark screen uses the bookmark entry's `government` (all Familial bookmark characters set `familial_government`; the bookmark decoration has no faith to check, so a non-Jewish bookmark family would show the kippah) for the shape and decoration; a bookmark character isn't a real character, so the Exilarchs are matched by bookmark name (`EXILARCH_BOOKMARKS` in the generator: Hasdai II and Boaz I in 867; add new ones there). Save games always show the kippah (no faith to check). Sabian heads get the crescent of Sin and Mandaean heads the klila (both 96x70, like the Keter; `jip_sabian_topframe_<size>.dds`, `jip_mandaean_topframe_<size>.dds`, `jip_coa_is_sabian/mandaean`), added by the generator once those textures exist (`FAITH_DECOR`). The government icon (`familial_government.dds`) is for every family; Jewish families' text shows the Mishpacha icon instead (`@government_type_mishpacha!`, `jip_mishpacha_government.dds`, through `GetGovernmentIcon`)

### Realm and migration
- Families are vassals of the realm their estate lies in, under the "Charter of Privileges" contract (communal taxes, religious rights, Beit Din and trade rights); Muslim lieges may levy jizya
- Moving the estate to another realm switches liege; AI families migrate to more welcoming realms on their own
- The engine only sets a travelling estate down where `can_move_domicile` (`common/scripted_rules/jip_scripted_rules.txt`) allows it, a city for Mishpacha, so a trip ending elsewhere used to leave it in the old realm. On arrival `jewish_admin_migration_on_action` first runs `jip_mishpacha_settle_estate_on_arrival_effect` (the city reached, else the realm's nearest city), then `jip_mishpacha_join_estate_realm_effect`. Mishpacha families skip the rule's war check; AI migration still waits out wars
- `jip_mishpacha_change_liege_effect` (`01_jip_liege_setup_effects.txt`) makes the family a vassal of a king or emperor, but a tributary (`tributary_settled`) of a duke or lesser ruler, since a duchy-tier family title can't be a duke's vassal (ranks are compared with `tier_duchy`, not the head's `highest_held_title_tier`). It runs at game start for independent families (`jip_liege_effect_setup`) and on estate arrival in a new realm (`jewish_admin_migration_on_action`), which ends the old liege or suzerain tie
- Vanilla's `yearly_playable_pulse` destroys the family titles of independent rulers who hold nothing else, which tributaries are too. `common/on_action/yearly_on_actions.txt` is a full copy of vanilla's file (an on_action's effect can't be appended to) with one `NOT = { government_has_flag = government_is_familial }` added; it's generated by `make_yearly_on_actions_override.py`, so rerun that after every vanilla update
- A head who leaves the family's religion steps down for the nearest man of the house who keeps it (`jip_family_left_faith_on_action`; the religion is `var:jip_family_religion` on the family title, set by `jip_familial_record_religion_effect` at game start and yearly); Family Succession (`mishpacha_succession_law`) covers family titles and Exilarchs
- Game start (`jip_jewish_families_game_start_on_action` in on_game_start, before the lobby, Roads to Power only): `jip_spawn_jewish_families_effect` creates up to 60 new families from per-region quotas (`common/scripted_effects/01_jip_jewish_family_spawn_effects.txt`), one per county, in cities of Christian, Muslim or Jewish realms that haven't expelled the Jews (`jip_jewish_family_home_county_trigger`), weighted by development, royal seats and Jewish counties. Each head (`jip_jewish_family_head_template`, aged 20–40, new dynasty) gets the family title and estate the same way as a dispossessed lord, the local ruler as liege or suzerain, and vanilla's `game_rule_create_spouse_and_children`. Culture follows the region, faith is mostly Rabbinic (some Merkabah; Karaites in Egypt, the Caliphate, Byzantium and the Khazar steppe). Root is empty in on_game_start, so nothing in this chain may use root. The families are made by `jip_create_familial_family_effect` (any culture, faith and rite saved as scopes); the same on_action then calls `jip_spawn_gnostic_families_effect` (`01_jip_gnostic_family_spawn_effects.txt`): two Sabian families (Aramean, rite `sabianism`) among Harran, Raqqa and Edessa before 1100, three Mandaean (Nabati, rite `mandeaism`) among Tib, the Marshes, Basra, Wasit and Ahvaz
- Thabit ibn Qurra (`jip_sabian_thabit_ibn_qurra`, `history/characters/jip_sabians.txt`) heads the Sabian family `d_jip_harrani` (al-Harrani, capital Baghdad) at 867, the Caliph's vassal (`jip_867_liege_setup_effect`); the start of the Sabian storyline. He is not on the bookmark screen (the mod's 867 bookmark is the Jewish World): play him from the map

### Titles
- Jewish family heads are **Mar/Geveret** and their spouses Geveret/Mar, whatever the liege (`top_liege = no`, priority 302, `religions = { judaism_religion }`); other families' heads and spouses are Mar/Marta (priority 301); estates are "the X Household"
- Jewish family titles use the `duchy_mishpacha` tier name ("Mishpacha"), others `duchy_familial` ("Bayta"); without one the landless titles log "Failed to find any valid flavorization"
- Exilarch and Kohen Gadol (priority 310) outrank Mar
- Title flavorization `flag` checks did not work for a Davidic "Nasi" variant; it was dropped

### Estate buildings (`common/domiciles/buildings/jip_mishpacha_estate_buildings.txt`)
- New branches with custom icons: **Yeshiva** (library), **Beit Din** (office), **Beit Knesset** (temple; slows migration, discourages expulsion)
- **Counting House** replaces the market line; **Hachnasat Orchim** replaces the Guest Room (piety instead of influence); both keep vanilla names/icons
- Influence-free copies replace vanilla Temple/Crypt/Prison/Trophy Room/Silk/Office lines; the vanilla versions are hidden in `jip_vanilla_estate_overrides.txt`, and existing estates are swapped by `jip_mishpacha_replace_estate_buildings_effect`
- The Villa's influence becomes prestige (`jip_mishpacha_villa_1..5` modifiers)
- Sabian and Mandaean branches (`jip_gnostic_estate_buildings.txt`, generated by `tools/make_gnostic_estate_buildings.py` from the Jewish ones; rerun after changing them): Sabian Diwan (as the Beit Din) and Haykal (as the Beit Knesset), Mandaean Scriptorium (as the Yeshiva) and Mandi (as the Beit Knesset), gated by `jip_can_build_sabian/mandaean_estate_building_trigger` (`jip_is_sabian/mandaean_family_trigger`: the flag and the rite). The Sabians' library branch is vanilla's Observatory. The Beit Knesset's ties and expulsion effects check its own keys, so they stay Jewish. Icons `jip_domicile_building_<name>.dds` (`make_domicile_icon.py` from a Gemini black silhouette; the generator falls back to a vanilla icon while one is missing, so rerun it after adding one). A vanilla Church or Monastery being replaced becomes the family's own branch (`jip_add_family_temple_branch_effect`), else the Crypt

### Court positions (`common/court_positions/types/jip_jewish_court_positions.txt`, generated)
- Mishpacha heads can appoint all eight: **Rosh Yeshiva, Av Beit Din, Paytan, Chazan, Gabbai Tzedakah, Shaliach, Sofer, Parnas**
- Landed Jewish rulers (duke and above) only get **Av Beit Din** and **Shaliach**, which no vanilla position covers for them; the rest duplicate vanilla positions they already have
- Holders must be adult Jews; the Yeshiva, Beit Din, Beit Knesset and Counting House raise the matching position's aptitude, shown as building parameters
- `minimum_rank` is a hard engine gate: positions above county also check `highest_held_title_tier` in `valid_position`, or appointments silently fail
- Tasks: positions with a vanilla counterpart share its tasks through `common/court_positions/tasks/jip_vanilla_task_overrides.txt` (exact vanilla copies with our positions added to `court_position_types` and the vanilla position's DLC added to `is_shown`; keep in sync with vanilla). New tasks are in `jip_jewish_court_position_tasks.txt`. Both files are generated
- Vanilla tasks that look up their own position by name (Educate Children, Organize Court) can't be shared: they would show but do nothing
- All eight and the Court Financier are full-time jobs (`jip_jewish_court_position_validity_trigger`): one per character, and not alongside a vanilla full-time position. Vanilla positions don't check ours, so a landed ruler's Av Beit Din or Shaliach could still take a vanilla job
- At game start (`jip_mishpacha_game_start_courtiers_on_action`) `jip_mishpacha_generate_courtiers_effect` adds a scholar, steward and envoy when no adult Jew at court has 10+ in that skill, and a cantor if the court still has fewer than four adult Jews (templates in `common/scripted_character_templates/jip_jewish_courtier_templates.txt`)
- Sabian and Mandaean families get copies under their own names, for holders of their rite (`jip_gnostic_court_positions.txt` and `jip_gnostic_court_position_tasks.txt`, generated by the same two scripts from `tools/jip_court_flavours.py`, which maps each Jewish position to its counterpart and lists the text replacements): Sabian Mu'allim, Ra'is al-Kahana, Sha'ir, Kahin, Almoner, Rasul, Katib, Wakil; Mandaean Ganzibra, Tarmida, Shganda, Izgadda, Sapra, Wakil (no judge or poet). They share their counterparts' vanilla tasks and get copies of their new tasks (not the Parnas' petition, which is about expulsion); opinion modifiers use `dualism_religion_opinion`. Position icons are vanilla placeholders (`gfx/interface/icons/court_position_types/jip_<key>_court_position.dds`); task icons (`court_position_task_types/<task>.dds`) are copied by the task generator from their Jewish counterparts' (`ICON_FROM` picks another: the Mu'allim's from the Astrologer's Compile a Zij)
- The Sofer combines the Seneschal and Chronicler (stewardship and diplomacy aptitude; the Chronicler's legend tasks plus Keep the Estate's Affairs)

### Contracts (`common/task_contracts/jip_mishpacha_contracts.txt`)
- Vanilla task contracts aren't tied to adventurers (admin and mandala rulers own them too), so Mishpacha heads own real contracts; only generation is ours. `jip_mishpacha_refresh_contracts_effect` calls `populate_task_contracts_for_area` on the estate with `jip_mishpacha_contracts_group`: yearly, on estate arrival, on a new head, at game start
- Jewish, Sabian and Mandaean families (`jip_familial_contract_family_trigger`); the Caravan (Radhanite roads) and the Responsum are Jewish only (`jip_is_jewish_family_trigger` in their `valid_to_create`). The shared texts name the family's faith through custom loc keyed on the head (`common/customizable_localization/jip_familial_custom_loc.txt`: `GetPlayer` in the contract window, `ROOT.Char` in the events, e.g. `JipFaithfulOne` Jew/Sabian/Mandaean, `JipFaithTongue`, `JipRansomDuty`; Jewish is the fallback); event texts are edited in `make_contract_events.py`. Ransom captives are of the family's faith (`jip_contract_ransomable_captive_trigger = { OWNER }`). The positions and branches that raise the odds count each faith's counterpart (`jip_employs_family_scribe/envoy/agent/teacher_trigger`, `jip_domicile_learning_branch_1/2_trigger`: Yeshiva, Scriptorium, or a Sabian's Observatory), and the scheme odds name them (`JipFamily*Name`)
- Player heads with Roads to Power only (`jip_mishpacha_contract_owner_trigger`); AI heads get a hidden yearly roll (`jip_mishpacha_ai_contract_effect`)
- Offers expire through the `jip_contract_fresh` variable (395 days) in `valid_to_keep`; `use_diplomatic_range = yes`, `travel = no`
- Nine non-martial contracts. `make_contract_events.py` generates their events (`events/jip_mishpacha_contract_events.txt`), the contract scheme types (`common/schemes/scheme_types/jip_mishpacha_contract_schemes.txt`), the schemes' approach modifiers (`common/modifiers/jip_contract_scheme_modifiers.txt`) and the event and scheme localization
- **Scheme contracts** (Caravan, Envoy, Ransom, Translate, News), like vanilla's laamp contract schemes: `.00X0` fires on acceptance and each approach starts a scheme (one type per contract and skill, `jip_contract_<contract>_<skill>_scheme`), sets its agent slots (success, balanced or speed mix) and adds approach modifiers (odds, pace). Phases and HUD clicks go through vanilla's preparations window (`scheme_critical_moments.0002`, via `jip_contract_scheme_prep_effect`), which executes into the hidden `.00X2`: it rolls the scheme's success chance (`jip_contract_roll_scheme_effect`) and fires `.00X3` (`window = scheme_successful_event`) or `.00X4` (`window = scheme_failed_event`), whose option runs `jip_contract_finish_effect` and ends the scheme. Estate and court bonuses are scripted modifiers in `common/scripted_modifiers/jip_contract_scheme_modifiers.txt`. A scheme that becomes invalid invalidates its contract, and an invalidated contract ends its scheme
- **Event contracts** (Loan, Tolls, Physician, Responsum): `.00X0` fires 2–4 months after acceptance: each option is an approach calling `jip_contract_choose_approach_effect = { SKILL BONUS APPROACH EVENT }`, which rolls the outcome (critical 30% / success 70% of the chance, failure the rest; chances are `jip_contract_<skill>_chance_value`, raised by the matching building and court position) and stores it with the approach on the contract. `.00X1` fires a few days later with text for that approach and outcome; its option runs `jip_contract_finish_effect`, i.e. the approach's own trade-off (`jip_contract_<contract>_approach_effect`) and then `complete_task_contract`
- In `task_contract_reward` effects `this` is the contract but `root` is whoever completed it (the event's character): reach the contract through `scope:task_contract`, never `root`
- One active contract, two with a Sofer; a Shaliach adds an offer per refresh. Loan and caravan stakes are paid on acceptance and refunded on invalidation

Title flavorizations also include: Emperor, King, Duke (Israelite heritage), Exilarch, Kohen Gadol, Kohen (chaplain position).

## Jews in Gentile Realms

- **Expel the Jews / Readmit the Jews** decisions for Christian and Muslim rulers, with AI weighting from traits and tenets; expelled lords may resist in one shared war and become Mishpacha families if they lose
- **Court Financier** court position for non-Jewish dukes and above: domain taxes and Jewish opinion, angers zealots, debt-collection events
- **Shtadlanut** (`common/character_interactions/jip_shtadlan_interactions.txt`, generated by `make_shtadlan_interactions.py`): four requests a landless Mishpacha head puts to a ruler in diplomatic range: Readmit the Jews (lifts the edict despite its cooldown), Letter of Protection (`jip_jews_protected` on the primary title for 15 years; blocks Expel the Jews, raises the migration host score), Release a Prisoner (kin first) and Ease the Charter (liege only: waive jizya, else lower communal taxes). Gold, prestige, hook and offer-a-favour send options (the last gives the ruler a `favor_hook` on the family, only if they hold no hook on it) are paid only on acceptance. AI acceptance is `jip_shtadlan_ai_accept_modifier`: request difficulty (`jip_shtadlan_<request>_difficulty_value`), minus `jip_jewish_hostility_value` (the Expel the Jews weights), half the ruler's opinion, faith hostility to Judaism, rank (`jip_shtadlan_rank_value`), not being the family's liege or suzerain, a favour granted in the last 10 years (`jip_shtadlan_granted` on the ruler), a Jewish Court Financier, a petitioning Parnas, and the sweeteners (a strong hook guarantees it). The AI never sends them

## The Exilarchate

A central political mechanic of the mod. Two rival figurehead titles:
- **d_exilarchate** — Rabbinic Exilarchate, held by the Davidite/Bostanite/Nathanite lines
- **d_karaite_exilarchate** — Karaite Exilarchate, held by the Ananite line

Both use **Exilarchate Elective** succession: dynasty members and claimants of the same faith vote. Players can use the **Claim Exilarchate** decision to start a scheme, gain a claim, become an elector, and campaign for the title. The Exilarchate has deep history files going back to -596.1.1 (the Babylonian exile).

## The Davidic Line Trait

**Type:** Fame trait, inheritable  
**Inheritance:** Male line only, 100% chance, does not inherit from real father (illegitimate children excluded)  
**Shown in ruler designer:** No — bloodline only

### Stat Effects
- +5 zealot opinion
- +10 same-faith opinion
- +0.2 monthly prestige
- Initial legitimacy boost flag

### Interaction with the Exilarchate
In Exilarchate elections, candidates with `davidic_line` receive a bonus to elector votes, scaled by each elector's zeal. Devout electors weight Davidic descent heavily, making the trait a major political asset when campaigning for the Exilarch title.

### Davidic Line Legacy Track
Unlocked when the dynasty dynast carries the `davidic_line` trait (or has already purchased into the track). Five perks, thematically mirroring David's legacy:

| Perk | Name | Effects |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | Traditional Slingers | Skirmisher MaA: +2 size, +20 damage, +20 pursuit, toughness/screen multipliers |
| 2 | David's Virility | +1 seduction scheme slot, +25% fertility, +5 years of fertility |
| 3 | Shepherd Kings | +2 Diplomacy, +0.5 monthly piety, +10 vassal opinion |
| 4 | Solomon's Wisdom | +3 Learning, +1 language-learning scheme slot |
| 5 | Clever Administrators | +50% development growth, +1 domain limit, +20% tax income |

## Mod branding (DLC-style icon and event banner)

**When creating events for this mod, always add the DLC banner** to every event that opens a window, right after its `theme` line:
```
	widgets = {
		widget = {
			gui = "jip_event_source_badge"
			container = "foreground_shader_vfx_container"
		}
	}
```
(Skip hidden events and events with `window = scheme_*_event`. Generators that write events must emit it.)

A mod can't be a DLC or an event's `content_source`, so:
- **Icon** `gfx/interface/icons/dlc/jip_rising_lion.dds` (120x120): a Gemini painting in vanilla's flavour-pack frame (Northern Lords, Fate of Iberia, Legacy of Persia share it), made by `make_dlc_icon.py <painting> <x0> <y0> <size>`; used as the texticon `@rising_lion_icon!`. It is deliberately not in the DLC rows of the main menu, bookmark screen or lobby: reaching them means copying vanilla GUI (all of `frontend_main.gui`, or the `button_show_dlc_list` type) that would need updating with every game patch
- **Event banner** `gfx/interface/illustrations/jip_event_source_banner.dds` (526x128, shown at 263x64): a Gemini painting in place of the art of vanilla's `dlc_009` event decoration, keeping its fade, ledge and shadow (`make_dlc_banner.py <painting> <y0>`). Every visible event adds `widgets = { widget = { gui = "jip_event_source_badge" container = "foreground_shader_vfx_container" } }` (`gui/event_window_widgets/jip_event_source_badge.gui`); new events need it too (`add_event_source_badge.py` adds it to all of them; `make_contract_events.py` emits it). The scheme outcome windows (`window = scheme_*_event`) and letter events have no such container

## Compatibility

- Compatible with **Cultures Expanded** mod (v0.10.1+)
- Compatible with **Aligned Realm Banners** (workshop 3823433423), which replaces the same `gui/shared/coat_of_arms.gui`: ours is built on its copy, so with both enabled Rising Lion must load after it and keeps ARB's overlay alignment of top decorations and its march/palatinate/castellan decorations. The file must work without ARB too, so its references are guarded: ARB's scripted GUIs are replaced by our copies (`common/scripted_guis/jip_arb_compat_scripted_guis.txt`), shown only when ARB's localization `setting_arb_contract_decor_on` resolves, and ARB's textures are chosen with `Select_CString` with a vanilla fallback. ARB's own game rule can't be read (it doesn't exist without ARB), so its decorations show whatever that rule is set to
- Integrates with the base game's **Persian Struggle** (Rabbinism and Karaism listed as involved faiths)
- Integrates with base game tributary setup system
