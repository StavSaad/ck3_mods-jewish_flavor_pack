displaced, the lost heirs of the Maccabees, Eldad ha-Dani; and, after 1.21, how the Familial government lives

## 3. A Hasmonean heir

A medieval house descended from the Maccabees: Hanukkah's legitimacy, and a claimant to the priest-kingship.

**The record**: `history/characters/jip_hasmonean.txt` runs from Yochanan the Kohen (-243) to Agrippa II's
generation. The longest line is Alexander's (`alexander_yanai_iii`, d. -7): Tigranes V and Alexander →
Tigranes VI of Armenia → Gaius Julius Alexander, king in Cilicia → Gaius Julius Alexander Berenicianus, consul
116. Josephus says this branch left Jewish ways (*Antiquities* 18.139-141). Nothing is documented after the 2nd
century.

**Decided**: the documented line plus a generated bridge, as its own house within `herodian_dynasty`; no trait,
no claimed-descent mechanic.

- [ ] Add Alexander's branch to `jip_hasmonean.txt` down to Berenicianus (Kokkinos; check each date): Alexander
  and Glaphyra → Tigranes V (childless) and Alexander → Tigranes VI → Gaius Julius Alexander and Iotapa of
  Commagene → Gaius Julius Agrippa, Berenicianus, Julia Iotapa. Hellenic (`hellenic_pagan`) and Greek from
  Tigranes V
- [ ] `make_hasmonean_line.py`: about 25 seeded generations from 116 to 867; one heir each, born when the father
  is 25-35, with one to three siblings who die childless
  - 116-300: senators and Galatian notables, Hellenic (Alexandros, Tigranes, Agrippas, Berenikianos, Iotape,
    Berenike; a Mariamme once a century)
  - 300-600: Christian; curials and bishops' kin of Ancyra
  - 600-867: the theme's soldiers, through the sacks of Ancyra (622, 838), where the memory nearly dies
- [ ] The house (`house_berenikianos`, `common/dynasty_houses/jip_houses.txt`) starts with Alexander's line;
  Hasmonean content checks it
- [ ] **Return to the faith of the Maccabees**: a decision to convert and take up the heritage, with Byzantium's
  fallout
- [ ] **The priest-kingship**: a Jewish heir holding Jerusalem (and the Temple, once rebuilt) may hold
  `d_kohen_gadol` with the crown
- [ ] **Josippon** (10th century, southern Italy): an event when it appears; prestige for the house

**Decided**: Alexandros Berenikianos, Greek and Orthodox at 867 (an earlier return would
skip the story's first acts), of Ancyra (`b_ankyra`, `c_galatia`; OGIS 544 names a Julius Severus of Ancyra as
kin of "King Alexander": check), a landless administrative house with an estate in the Bucellarian theme (Roads
to Power; without it a courtier of the strategos). The family keeps a signet with a palm branch and a Hebrew
legend no one can read.

## 4. Stories: the Hasmoneans and the Exilarchs

Built like the Sabian story and vanilla's `ep3_story_cycle_hasan_sabbah`.

### Shared
- [ ] One story type per line (`common/story_cycles/jip_*_story_cycle.txt`); a house story passes to the heir,
  a title story to the next holder
- [ ] The stage is a story variable; yearly effect groups fire its events; visible decisions in the story's own
  group for the player's milestones
- [ ] Shared building blocks (installation, crisis, the caliph's favour) as scripted effects
- [ ] The story window (`make_story_window_override.py`) shows each story's act, a 0-10 counter, a patron and a
  list of people: one block per story in the generator, no new GUI
- [ ] Counters (changed only through raise/lower effects): the Caliph's favour (Rabbinic), the Mourners of Zion
  (Karaite), the claim's strength (Beit Nathan), the memory (Hasmonean: 0 ends Act I, 10 opens the return)
- [ ] **AI owners** (decided): the four stories run quietly for AI owners (no events shown, outcomes
  rolled), so a player meets a living rival line

### The Geonim
- [ ] **Gaon of Sura** and **Gaon of Pumbedita** (`jip_gaon_sura_court_position`,
  `jip_gaon_pumbedita_court_position`; decided: positions, not titles): the Rabbinic Exilarch's only (also a
  merchant Exilarch, 14a); a learned adult Rabbinic Jew (learning 14+), for life; dismissal is a crisis, not a
  click
- [ ] Filled at game start for Hasdai II from generated scholars (the named Geonim begin about 871-872);
  refilled by event when one dies (the academy sends two candidates)
- [ ] An ambitious or arrogant Gaon under a weak Exilarch (low legitimacy or favour) starts the quarrel
- [ ] **Saadia** (882-942): born in the Fayyum (`c_fayyum`), a family courtier in Egypt; about 915-928 the
  Exilarch is offered "a scholar from Egypt" as Gaon of Sura. Learning 25+, `stubborn`, `zealous`. Without him a
  generated Gaon plays the part more mildly

### The feud of Beit Bustenai and Beit Anan
Anan ben David, passed over for his brother (c. 760s), founded the sect that became the Karaites.
- [ ] A vanilla house relation at feud level, set at game start (`set_house_relation_level_effect`), reason
  `house_relation_reason_jip_anan_passed_over_desc`. Feud gives -30 opinion, hostile scheme bonus, the
  eradicate casus belli, fewer marriages and guests
- [ ] The stories move it (`change_house_relation_level`): deeper through polemics, rival Exilarchs, slights;
  easier through marriage, standing together against a caliph or a persecution, a shared Pesach. Full
  reconciliation is rare; the schism stays
- [x] Keys: the Ananites are `house_ananite`, a cadet house of `bostanite_dynasty`; the Bostanite main house has
  no key (reach it as `character:<Hasdai>.house`, saved at game start)
- [ ] Test: vanilla accepts a feud inside one dynasty, and the dynast's powers (disinherit, denounce) don't make
  it one-sided; if they do, close those interactions between the two houses

### `story_jip_exilarchate_rabbinic`
Owner: the holder of `d_exilarchate` (Hasdai II at 867). A reign's story, restarted at each installation.
- [ ] **Each reign**: Installation → the Court (favour moves) → at most one great crisis → a verdict at death (a
  nickname and a house modifier: "son of a beloved Exilarch", or of a deposed one)
- [ ] **Installation** (three events, after Nathan ha-Bavli): the Geonim's consent (a hostile Gaon names a
  price), the Sabbath homage (learning or diplomacy), the audience (the gift sets the starting favour; the
  vizier may want his own). An Exilarch outside the caliphate installs before whoever rules Baghdad, or with no
  patron and no counter
- [ ] **The Court's decisions**: Collect the Communities' Dues (gold against the families' opinion; the
  reshuyot), Appoint a Judge (to a Jewish county or a family's Beit Din), Seek an Audience (favour for gold or a
  hook)
- [ ] **Exilarch against Gaon**: the Gaon refuses to sign a judgment → bans both ways → a counter-Exilarch from
  the house (a claim on `d_exilarchate`) against a counter-Gaon → the caliph arbitrates for whoever has more
  favour, gold or friends → years later, reconciliation (937, at Purim). A family head in the caliphate is asked
  to take a side
- [ ] **Disfavour** at 0: fined, then banished; the estate (a merchant's domicile, 14a) moves to Kairouan
  (`c_kairwan`, as Mar Ukba's) or the player's choice outside the caliphate; a regent cousin stays in Baghdad;
  the way back is favour from abroad or a new caliph
- [ ] **Later starts**: the office weakens after 1040 (Hezekiah imprisoned) and revives in the 12th century

### `story_jip_exilarchate_karaite`
Owner: the holder of `d_karaite_exilarchate` (Boaz I at 867).
- [ ] **The Mourners of Zion**: al-Qumisi's call; the Nasi may move the community's centre to Jerusalem
- [ ] **Polemics** with Saadia and the Geonim (Salmon ben Jeroham's replies) as debate events
- [ ] **The calendar**: a yearly sighting of the new moon and the *aviv* barley in the Land
- [ ] **Missions** to Byzantium and Egypt; **1099**: books and captives ransomed, the centre moves to Egypt or
  Constantinople

### `story_jip_beit_nathan`: the lost crown
Owner: the head of Beit Nathan (`nathanite_dynasty`, Pinchas ben Abdimi's family at 867), the house of the
Exilarchs before Bostanai.
- [ ] The old claim kept alive by genealogies, elders and Geonim
- [ ] The Bostanai controversy (the Persian princess's sons called slaves) as a weapon against the Bostanite
  holder
- [ ] The road back: Claim Exilarchate, alliance with a Gaon in the Rabbinic quarrel, courting electors
- [ ] Another crown: a Davidic *nesiut* elsewhere (Mosul, Damascus, Egypt; the Nagid, section 9)
- [ ] Ends when Beit Nathan regains `d_exilarchate`, founds its nesiut, or dies out

### `story_jip_hasmonean_return`
Owner: the head of `house_berenikianos` (section 3).
1. **Memory**: a family tradition in Byzantium that grows or fades each generation; if it fades, the story ends
2. **Seeking**: a Jewish teacher, secret study; from the mid-10th century the *Josippon*
3. **The return**: section 3's decision; Basil I's forced baptisms (873) make Byzantium dangerous: flee to
   Khazaria (marriage into the Khazar house), the caliphate or southern Italy
4. **The Land**: settle in the Land of Israel; win or be granted Jerusalem
5. **Rededication**: the Temple, Hanukkah in Jerusalem, crown and `d_kohen_gadol`

### Reconciliation: one Israel again
In Fatimid Egypt Rabbanites and Karaites cooperated and intermarried (Rustow). Last in the build order.
- [ ] **Preconditions**: the feud eased to cordial or better; both houses and both Exilarchates exist
- [ ] **Mixed marriages** may carry a double contract (the Geniza contracts), easing the relation
- [ ] **The assembly**: a decision for either Exilarch; a debate chain (learning, diplomacy, piety, the
  attendees' opinion and zeal) decides it. AI Exilarchs agree only with high opinion and low zeal
- [ ] **Outcomes**: (1) **union of rites**, when the two are close in strength: the smaller faith's rite joins
  the larger (`set_parent_faith = { target = <faith> main = no include_derived = yes }`), both titles survive;
  (2) **merger**, when one side is clearly stronger: the minority converts over time (`set_county_rite`, zealots
  refusing or keeping it secret), the losing line's title becomes ceremonial; (3) **failure**: the relation falls
  back, a cooldown starts
- [ ] A script value measuring dominance (counties, followers, holy sites), shown in the decision's tooltip
- [ ] A renewed schism through `detach_rite_to_new_faith` if a union falls back into feud
- [ ] Test in game: what the emptied faith does; whether moving a rite swaps its tenets or doctrines

## F. Familial gameplay (1.21)

The Familial government's own mechanics, its own build track: the government's rules and army, the HUD, the
patron, the conversions to and from companies and adventurers, the custom start, buildings, contracts and trade
routes. Everything here applies to every family, Jewish, Sabian or Mandaean, and ties to no story; the Jewish
and story pieces (merchants as Exilarch, the Radhanites, the Jewish quarter) are in section 14.

Silk & Silver's Merchant is the Familial shape in vanilla: a landless house in a city under a patron. 0.15.1
built the conversion both ways; 0.16 decides how the two meet. 0.15.1's open items for 0.16 (its sections 4, 5,
6 and 9) are folded in here and in section 14.

### What 1.21 does (read from the game files, 2026-11-24)
- **`merchant_government`** (`00_government_types.txt`): rules `landless_playable`, `profit_trade_routes = yes`,
  `distribution_trade_routes = no`, `can_have_vassal_suzerain = yes`, `can_sponsor_construction = yes`; domicile
  `merchant_company`; county-tier title; flags `cannot_be_vassal_or_liege`, `government_is_merchant`,
  `government_has_merchant_company`. Modifiers: **`men_at_arms_cap = -99`** (no men-at-arms at all), mercenaries
  +100%, travel speed +20%, prestige gain -50%
- **Ours** (`jip_familial_government_types.txt`): MaA allowed (upkeep +50%), mercenaries -25%, income +15%,
  diplomatic range x2, legitimacy, noble families, `council = no`, estate domicile, duchy-tier title, vassal
  contract `mishpacha_vassal`
- **The patron**: the group `tributary_merchant` (`subject_contract_groups.txt`) is `is_tributary` and
  `is_beneficiary_relationship`, can't be broken from below, passes to both heirs, and its contracts are
  `merchant_taxes` and `merchant_prestige` (plus two shown only to merchants). It is valid only for
  `is_landless_merchant = yes` with a landed suzerain, so **families can't use it as it is**. The patron is
  whoever holds the company's county, set by script (`start_tributary_interaction_effect`); moving the company
  swaps patron (`gdp_move_company_departure_effect`). Nothing in it checks a DLC
- **Become Merchant** (`become_merchant_decision`, `gdp_merchant_decisions.txt`): `has_gdp_dlc_trigger`; for
  adventurers, republics, landed counts and dukes; the conversion runs in the event `gdp_merchant.0001`, not
  one scripted effect; 5 years of `gdp_voluntary_merchant_modifier`; all prestige lost
- **Merchants can be evicted**: vanilla's eviction picks a new patron outside the expelling realm, an overlap
  with our expulsion
- **The council tab** (`hud.gui`, `tab_council`) is hidden by a hard check, `Not( Or( IsLandlessAdventurer(
  GetPlayer ), IsLandlessMerchant( GetPlayer ) ) )`: no flag or rule reaches it. The trade tab shows for
  `GetGovernment.HasRule( 'profit_trade_routes' )` or a landed ruler
- **Custom landless starts are bookmark data, not GUI**: a bookmark character takes `character_design_type =
  landless_adventurer | noble_family | merchant` and a `government` (`00_bookmarks.txt`: the custom adventurer
  of 867, the Chinese noble family of 1066, the Silk Road merchant of 1178), and the bookmark screen shows it as
  a "new" character opening the ruler designer (`StartDesignLandlessNobleFamily`, `IsDesigningLandless`)
