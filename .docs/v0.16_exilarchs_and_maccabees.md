## 3. A Hasmonean heir

A medieval house descended from the Maccabees: Hanukkah's legitimacy, and a claimant to the priest-kingship.

**The record**: `history/characters/jip_hasmonean.txt` runs from Yochanan the Kohen (-243) to Agrippa II's
generation. The longest line is Alexander's (`alexander_yanai_iii`, d. -7): Tigranes V and Alexander →
Tigranes VI of Armenia → Gaius Julius Alexander, king in Cilicia → Gaius Julius Alexander Berenicianus, consul
116. Josephus says this branch left Jewish ways (*Antiquities* 18.139-141). Nothing is documented after the 2nd
century.

**Decided**: the documented line plus a generated bridge, as its own house within `herodian_dynasty`; no trait,
no claimed-descent mechanic.

- [ ] Add Alexander's branch to `jip_hasmonean.txt` down to Berenicianus (Kokkinos; check each date): Alexander
  and Glaphyra → Tigranes V (childless) and Alexander → Tigranes VI → Gaius Julius Alexander and Iotapa of
  Commagene → Gaius Julius Agrippa, Berenicianus, Julia Iotapa. Hellenic (`hellenic_pagan`) and Greek from
  Tigranes V
- [ ] `make_hasmonean_line.py`: about 25 seeded generations from 116 to 867; one heir each, born when the father
  is 25-35, with one to three siblings who die childless
  - 116-300: senators and Galatian notables, Hellenic (Alexandros, Tigranes, Agrippas, Berenikianos, Iotape,
    Berenike; a Mariamme once a century)
  - 300-600: Christian; curials and bishops' kin of Ancyra
  - 600-867: the theme's soldiers, through the sacks of Ancyra (622, 838), where the memory nearly dies
- [ ] The house (`house_berenikianos`, `common/dynasty_houses/jip_houses.txt`) starts with Alexander's line;
  Hasmonean content checks it
- [ ] **Return to the faith of the Maccabees**: a decision to convert and take up the heritage, with Byzantium's
  fallout
- [ ] **The priest-kingship**: a Jewish heir holding Jerusalem (and the Temple, once rebuilt) may hold
  `d_kohen_gadol` with the crown
- [ ] **Josippon** (10th century, southern Italy): an event when it appears; prestige for the house

**Decided**: Alexandros Berenikianos, Greek and Orthodox at 867 (an earlier return would
skip the story's first acts), of Ancyra (`b_ankyra`, `c_galatia`; OGIS 544 names a Julius Severus of Ancyra as
kin of "King Alexander": check), a landless administrative house with an estate in the Bucellarian theme (Roads
to Power; without it a courtier of the strategos). The family keeps a signet with a palm branch and a Hebrew
legend no one can read.

## 4b. Build steps 2 and 3 in detail: the feud, the Geonim, Se'adyah, the Rabbinic Exilarch

What the code has today (checked):

- The story window already shows an act string, one 0-10 counter, one character and one character list natively

### Step 2a. The feud of Beit Bustenai and Beit Anan (#72)

#### Setup
- [ ] `jip_exilarch_feud_setup_effect` (on_game_start, after the lobby; new file `01_jip_exilarch_effects.txt`):
  saves the Bostanite main house as the global `jip_house_bustenai` (from the holder of `d_jip_bostanite`, else
  `character:rabbinic_exilarchate_hasdai_ii.house`) and `house:house_ananite` as `jip_house_anan`; sets the relation
  to **feud**, reason `jip_anan_passed_over` (desc: "Anan ben David was passed over for his brother Chananyah, and
  went out to found a sect of his own").
- [ ] Only where both houses exist with living heads; at 1066 and 1178 too (same keys).

#### Keeping it alive
- [ ] **Range pruning**: vanilla drops the relation once the heads are out of diplomatic range (a Karaite centre moved
  to Yerushalayim would do it). Fix: the yearly pulse stores the level on the global (`var:jip_schism_level`, -3..+3)
  and re-sets it if the relation vanished. No vanilla override.
- [ ] **Drift**: vanilla drifts relations to neutral. The schism shouldn't heal by forgetting: the yearly pulse holds
  a floor of **quarrel** unless a reconciliation step has been taken (`var:jip_schism_eased`, set by the easing events
  below; the floor is lifted for 20 years). Only the Reconciliation story (build step 8) can take it above cordial.

#### What moves it (`jip_schism_change_effect = { STEPS REASON }`, which updates the variable too)
Deeper:
- [ ] `jip_exilarch_feud.0100` **A tract against the Rabbanites** (yearly roll, ~10%, Karaite Exilarch with learning
  10+): a Karaite polemic reaches Baghdad. The Rabbinic Exilarch answers it (learning check: piety, and the feud
  deepens a step), has the Gaon answer it (the Gaon's prestige, no step), or lets it pass (stress for zealous).
  Mirror event `.0101` for the Karaite side's answer to a Rabbinic tract.
- [ ] `.0110` **Precedence at the audience** (when both Exilarchs share a patron): whose Exilarch stands first before
  the Caliph. Push it (prestige, the feud deepens), yield (prestige loss, eases nothing), or pay the chamberlain
  (gold, no step).
- [ ] `.0120` **The calendar**: the two communities keep the feasts on different days this year (the Karaites by the
  sighted moon, the Rabbanites by the fixed reckoning). Flavour with a small choice; a step deeper only if a zealot
  Exilarch mocks the other's feast.
- [ ] Rival Exilarchs: either side pressing a claim on the other's title, or a counter-Exilarch backed by the other
  house (step 3), deepens it two steps.
Easier:
- [ ] `.0200` **A match between the houses** (marriage between members of the two houses): eases a step, sets
  `jip_schism_eased`. Hook: on_marriage, both houses checked; text names the Ketubah written for both laws (the
  Geniza's double contracts), which is the seed for step 8.
- [ ] `.0210` **A common front**: when a ruler of the realm the Exilarchs live in takes Expel the Jews, or the
  Question of the Sabians-style pressure falls on the Jews (`jip_jewish_hostility_value` high), the two Exilarchs
  may petition together: eases a step, and the Shtadlanut odds of the next Readmit request rise.
- [ ] `.0220` **A shared Pesach**: a new option in the seder's summary (5005) for an Exilarch host: invite the
  other house's head. Accepted (AI: opinion and low zeal) eases a step; refused deepens one.

#### Within one dynasty
- [ ] Test in game: a feud between a house and its own cadet house works, and the dynast's powers (disinherit,
  denounce) aren't one-sided. **Recommended fallback: accept it as it is** (the Bostanite dynast disinheriting an
  Ananite is the feud in action); closing vanilla's interactions would mean copying them by key, with upkeep every
  patch. Open question Q1.

#### AI
- [ ] The events fire for AI heads with `ai_chance` weighted by zeal, vengeful/forgiving and opinion; only a player
  on either side sees them. A toast tells Jewish players in range when the feud deepens to feud or eases to quarrel.

### Step 3. The Rabbinic Exilarch's story, `story_jip_exilarchate_rabbinic` (#70, #73)

#### Shape
Owner: the holder of `d_exilarchate`. **A reign's story**: it starts at game start (after the lobby) for Chasdai II and
on each on_title_gain of the title; it ends at the holder's death (the verdict) or when the title leaves him. Variables
on the story:
- `jip_exil_act`: 1 Installation, 2 the Court, 3 the Crisis, 4 Exile. The window's act string (`JipExilarchAct`).
- `jip_exil_favour` 0-10: **the Caliph's favour**, the window's counter. Changed only through
  `jip_exil_raise_favour_effect` / `jip_exil_lower_favour_effect = { VALUE = 1-3 }`.
- `jip_exil_patron`: the window's character: the Exilarch's top liege (`suzerain.top_liege`, i.e. whoever rules Baghdad's
  realm when the estate is there). Labelled "the Caliph" when he is the Sunni head of faith, else "the Sultan" or "the
  Ruler of Baghdad" (custom loc).
- `jip_exil_people`: the window's list: the Geonim, a counter-Exilarch, a counter-Gaon, a regent.
- `jip_exil_crisis_done`: set when the reign's one great crisis is over (only one per reign).
- Decisions in the story's own group `jip_story_exilarchate_rabbinic` (important), listed in the window.

**AI holders** (decided: quiet): the same events fire for them and the AI picks by `ai_chance` (no one sees AI events);
players are shown only what touches them (side-taking, a counter-Exilarch, a banishment) and a toast for each
crisis's outcome to Jewish players within range. No separate "rolled" path to maintain.

#### Shared framework (#70, built first)
- [ ] `common/story_cycles/jip_story_exilarchate_rabbinic.txt`, built like `jip_story_sabian_revival.txt`, with the
  daily act checks and yearly effect groups.
- [ ] Scripted effects in `01_jip_exilarch_story_effects.txt`: start/end, favour raise/lower, update patron, the people
  list, set act; and the blocks the Karaite and Beit Nathan stories will reuse: `jip_exil_installation_effect`,
  `jip_exil_crisis_start/end_effect`, `jip_exil_banish_effect`.
- [ ] On_actions `jip_exilarch_story_on_actions.txt`: game start, on_title_gain/on_title_lost of `d_exilarchate`,
  on the patron's death or the patron's primary title changing hands (a new caliph).
- [ ] No GUI override: the vanilla visualization already shows act, counter, character, list and decisions.

#### Act I: Installation (three events in the first year, after Nathan ha-Bavli's account)
- [ ] `jip_exilarch.0100` **The Geonim's consent**: each Gaon gives consent. A Gaon with opinion below 0 names a price:
  gold, a post for his son (a judgeship: the next Appoint a Judge goes to him), or a share of the dues. Pay, or refuse
  (his opinion falls; the quarrel trigger counts it). With a seat vacant, the candidates event fires instead.
- [ ] `.0110` **The Sabbath homage**: the canopy in the synagogue, the elders, the Exilarch's discourse on the week's
  portion. Give it yourself (learning: legitimacy and prestige, or a stumble), let the Gaon of Sura give it with
  praise for the elders (diplomacy: the Geonim's opinion), or a lavish feast (gold: prestige). 
- [ ] `.0120` **The audience** with the patron (the Thursday after; the Exilarch rides in the royal carriage): the gift
  sets the starting favour, modest 3, rich 5, splendid (gold or an artifact) 7; the vizier wants his own (pay: nothing;
  refuse: -1 favour and his opinion).
- [ ] **Outside the caliphate**: if the patron isn't Muslim, or the estate is out of the realm that holds Baghdad,
  the audience is before whoever rules Baghdad's realm, or, with no patron at all, it is skipped and the counter
  removed (the window hides it, as the Sabian standing).
- [ ] Ends at `.0120` → Act II. For AI: the same three events.

#### Act II: The Court
**Favour over time**: yearly, favour moves one step toward a target set by the patron's opinion of the Exilarch
(-50 or less: 1; 0: 4; +50 or more: 8), and the events and decisions below push it.
- [ ] `jip_exilarch.0200` **A new caliph** (patron dies or loses the title): favour falls to the middle of the old
  value and 3; an accession gift as in the audience restores some.
- [ ] `.0210` **The vizier's price** (yearly roll): pay, refuse (-1), or expose him (intrigue: +1 and a hook on him).
- [ ] `.0220` **An accuser at court** (a courtier of the patron's who dislikes Jews; `jip_jewish_hostility_value`):
  answer with learning, gold, or a friend at court; -1 or -2 if ignored.
- [ ] `.0230` **A friend at court**: a Jewish physician or Court Financier at the patron's court speaks for you (+1).
- [ ] Decisions:
  - [ ] **Collect the Communities' Dues** (`jip_exilarch_collect_dues_decision`, every 3 years): gold for each Rabbinic
    family and Rabbinic county in the patron's realm (the reshut); every Rabbinic family head there gets
    `jip_exilarch_dues_opinion` (-10, 5 years), halved if both Geonim consent (their opinion 20+).
  - [ ] **Appoint a Judge** (`jip_exilarch_appoint_judge_decision`, every 2 years): a learned courtier of yours becomes
    Av Beit Din of a Rabbinic family in the reshut that has none (the family's opinion and your legitimacy), or a
    dayyan of a Rabbinic county (`jip_exilarch_dayyan` county modifier: opinion, development). Target chosen from the
    decision list widget, like the Sabian Claim a City. A judgment issued here can set off the Gaon's refusal (2b).
  - [ ] **Seek an Audience** (`jip_exilarch_seek_audience_decision`, every 2 years): gold (scaled to income) or a hook
    on the patron or his vizier: +1 favour, +2 with an artifact gift; fails at patron opinion below -30.

#### Act III: The great crisis (at most one per reign)
**A. Exilarch against Gaon** (Se'adyah against David ben Zakkai, 930-937). Starts when the Gaon refuses to sign and the
Exilarch insists (2b's `.0200`), or when a Gaon is dismissed (`.0300`).
- [ ] `jip_exilarch.0300` **The bans**: the Exilarch bans the Gaon; the Gaon bans the Exilarch and proclaims a
  **counter-Exilarch**: the nearest adult Davidic man of the Exilarch's house, not his heir, created if none (Yehoshiyahu
  ben Zakkai's part). The Exilarch names a **counter-Gaon** (a learned courtier, or the academy's second candidate) to the position.
  Both go in the window's list.
- [ ] **The counter-Exilarch's claim**: a pressed claim on `d_exilarchate`. If he wins, the title moves to him and the
  deposed Exilarch keeps only his family title (decided).
- [ ] **Every new Exilarch holds a family title** (decided; both Exilarchates, whatever the cause: this crisis, an
  election, a script, a heir who wasn't a ruler): `jip_exilarchate_familial_guard_effect`, on_title_gain of
  `d_exilarchate` and `d_karaite_exilarchate`. If the new holder isn't Familial or holds no `d_jip_*` family title, he
  gets a generated family title and an estate in Baghdad (or his court's city) by the dispossessed lord's steps
  (`jip_create_familial_family_effect`'s parts), and the Familial government. Runs before the story starts, so the
  story's owner is always a family head. Generalises PR #101's election limit, which stays.
- [ ] `.0310` **The communities take sides** (yearly while it lasts): each Rabbinic or Merkabah house head in the
  patron's realm takes a side: Familial heads, and also merchant companies and landless adventurers there (decided;
  `jip_exil_side_taker_trigger`: the faith, `government_is_familial`, `government_is_merchant` or a landless adventurer,
  and the estate, company or camp in the patron's realm); players get `.0315` (the Exilarch, the Gaon, or stay out: opinions and a small piety cost for staying
  out of a zealot's quarrel). The counts are stored on the story.
- [ ] `.0320` **The Caliph arbitrates** (two to four years in, or at once when either side pays): each side's score is
  favour x2, gold paid, supporters, legitimacy level, and the Gaon's learning/10.
  - Exilarch wins: the counter-Exilarch is banished to Khurasan (claim removed, moved out of the court), the Gaon
    deposed (he goes into hiding at a family's court), the counter-Gaon confirmed; legitimacy up, the Gaon's
    supporters' opinion down.
  - Gaon wins: the counter-Exilarch is installed (title moves; the old Exilarch keeps his family as a plain family
    head and his story ends with the "deposed" verdict); or, milder, the Exilarch keeps the title with legitimacy and
    favour lost, and must restore the Gaon.
- [ ] `.0330` **The reconciliation** (3-7 years after the verdict, if both live; the history's 937): at the Exilarch's
  **Purim** feast if he holds one in season, else in Adar as a household event: the Gaon restored, bans lifted,
  legitimacy and piety; the people list cleared. Ends the crisis.

**B. Disfavour** (favour reaches 0).
- [ ] `.0400` **The fine**: pay (gold scaled to income: favour back to 2) or refuse.
- [ ] `.0410` **Banishment** (refused, or favour at 0 again within 5 years): Act IV. The estate moves to Kairouan
  (`c_kairwan`, Ukba's road, if it is outside the patron's realm and a city is free) or to a city of the player's
  choice outside the realm (the estate's ordinary move, required within a year: `var:jip_exil_must_leave`; staying
  means prison). A **regent cousin** stays in Baghdad (listed in the window; nothing mechanical, he is the house's man
  at court). The counter is kept; Seek an Audience costs double from abroad.
- [ ] **The way back** (`jip_exilarch_return_decision`): favour 5 from abroad, or a new patron (`.0200` resets to 3 for
  an exile): the estate moves back to Baghdad, legitimacy, Act II, crisis done.

#### The verdict (at the holder's death)
- [ ] `jip_exilarch.0900` to the heir (hidden for AI): a score from legitimacy level, the favour at death, the crisis's
  outcome and banishment.
  - High: the nickname **the Beloved**, house modifier `jip_son_of_beloved_exilarch` (20 years: +legitimacy for the next

  - Low or deposed: **the Banished** / **the Deposed**, `jip_son_of_deposed_exilarch` (the next Exilarch's installation

  - Otherwise no nickname.

#### The academies: one design (decided: designed now, as a whole)
Sura, Pumbedita, the revived Yeshiva of Baghdad, the Yeshiva of Eretz Yisrael and the great yeshivot of the west are
one system with five shared parts, so each new academy is data, not new machinery.

**1. What an academy is.**
| Academy | Kept by | Its head |
|---|---|---|

**2. Heads come from the academy** (shared): the Gaon positions take only the academy's candidates, through one
candidates event, `jip_geonim.0100`, with the academy as a parameter. A great yeshiva's family head is its head

- **Found a Great Yeshiva** (`jip_found_great_yeshiva_decision`): any ruler, Jewish or not, with a Rabbinic family's

  list widget. AI weight: higher for learned, tolerant or greedy rulers and as Bavel declines (`jip_academies_closed`),
  lower for zealots and `jip_jewish_hostility_value`. History's seats (Kairouan about 1000, Mainz, Lucena about 1088,

- **Restore the Exilarch's Honours** is the same decision for the Yeshiva of Baghdad (above).
- **Closing**: Sura and Pumbedita by `jip_geonim.0500` (above); a great yeshiva closes when its family is expelled or
  leaves the realm (it may refound it where it settles: `jip_geonim.0510`, the academy moves with the scholars, as
  Kairouan's did), or when its family dies out. Expel the Jews closes every great yeshiva in the realm; Readmit doesn't

weighs on Sura and Pumbedita through the decline value), with no per-family accounting.

**5. Letters between academies** (flavour, shared): `jip_geonim.0600`-`0620`:

#### The Geonim as clergy, after vanilla's cardinals

**Revised 2026-11-25 evening** (Stav: not court positions, not Familial titles; ecclesiastical if anything). The model
is vanilla's College of Cardinals (By God Alone, read from the 1.21 files):
- a cardinal holds a landless duchy title (`d_cd_*`, or one made by `create_dynamic_title` and named after a temple
  barony, `grant_cardinal_title_effect`), registered with `add_clerical_elector = <faith>`;
- its holder takes `ecclesiastical_government` (landless playable, treasury, monthly piety, few men-at-arms), and the
  CoA shows the cardinal's decoration (`HasClericalElectorTitle`, in `coat_of_arms.gui`);
- the faith has the parameter `has_clerical_electors` (`special_doctrine_has_clerical_electors`), and an election can
  name them as electors with the character set type `same_faith_clerical_electors` (vanilla's `theocratic_elective`);
- vanilla fills vacancies only through the faith's head (`pam_cardinal_vacancy_on_death_effect` needs a
  `religious_head` or the Papal State), so for a faith with no head, like Rabbinism, nothing of vanilla's runs: the
  filling is ours.

#### The titles
- [ ] `d_gaonate_sura` (capital `c_kufa`, Sura beside it) and `d_gaonate_pumbedita` (capital `c_anbar`, or Baghdad,
  where both academies moved about 900: check the counties' keys), landless duchy titles like `d_exilarchate`, in
  `jip_landed_titles.txt`; the revived `d_gaonate_baghdad` the same (step 3); the Yeshiva of Eretz Yisrael's
  `d_palestinian_gaonate` (step 6) becomes one too; the great yeshivot (their own step) are dynamic titles named
  after their city, as vanilla's dynamic cardinalates are.
- [ ] Each registered as a clerical elector of the Rabbinic faith (`add_clerical_elector = faith:rabbinic_faith`)
  when given a holder, and the faith gets `special_doctrine_has_clerical_electors` in `jip_jewish_faith_types.txt`.
  To test: that vanilla's PAM scripts, which check that parameter (cardinal orders, the Christian church situation,
  the 867 College seeding), stay quiet for a faith with no head; and that a Merkabah Gaon counts as
  `same_faith_clerical_electors` with a Rabbinic Exilarch (same faith, other rite).
- [ ] Holder: a learned adult Rabbinic or Merkabah Jew (learning 14+); never the Exilarch himself.

#### Their government: `geonic_government` (ours, in vanilla's ecclesiastical shape)
Vanilla's `ecclesiastical_government` would be the shortest road, but it is Christian through and through (its
domicile is a church with Christian buildings, its budget is the Pope's cardinal budget, its tie is the Christian
Church situation) and it needs By God Alone to be played. So ours, beside the Familial in
`jip_familial_government_types.txt`:
- [ ] rules: `landless_playable` (Roads to Power, as families), `sticky_government`, no levies, legitimacy; flags
  `government_is_theocracy` (so vanilla's `pam_strip_illegal_cardinalate_effect` leaves the seat alone),
  `government_is_geonic`; mechanic type theocracy; `council = no` (our `hud.gui` already hides the tab).
- [ ] modifiers: +monthly piety and prestige, learning XP, no men-at-arms regiments to speak of (a Gaon has
  students, not soldiers), mercenaries dearer; income from the academy's dues (a yearly sum from the Rabbinic counties
  and families of the patron's realm).
- [ ] **the domicile is the academy**: vanilla's estate with our Familial lines (the Yeshiva, Beit Din, Beit Knesset,
  Counting House), renamed "the Academy of Sura" etc. in the domicile window; the Jewish court positions (Rosh
  Yeshiva, Av Beit Din, Sofer...) open to Geonim as to family heads.
- [ ] **the patron**: a Gaon is a tributary of his seat's county holder through `tributary_familial`, widened to
  `government_is_geonic` (the caliph's realm, as for the Exilarch). The Exilarch's hold over him is not a vassal tie
  but the election and the crisis.
- [ ] **the realm CoA**: a Gaon's decoration in our generated `coat_of_arms.gui` (in place of the cardinal's hat;
  Gemini art, a tallit-draped scroll or the rabbinic turban: art step).
- [ ] the government is the Geonim's only: a Gaon who loses his seat becomes a Familial head (his house keeps a family
  title, so Geonic houses like Sherira's and Hai's carry on), or a courtier if he had none.

## F. Familial gameplay (1.21)

The Familial government's own mechanics, its own build track: the government's rules and army, the HUD, the
patron, the conversions to and from companies and adventurers, the custom start, buildings, contracts and trade
routes. Everything here applies to every family, Jewish, Sabian or Mandaean, and ties to no story; the Jewish
and story pieces (merchants as Exilarch, the Radhanites, the Jewish quarter) are in section 14.

Silk & Silver's Merchant is the Familial shape in vanilla: a landless house in a city under a patron. 0.15.1
built the conversion both ways; 0.16 decides how the two meet. 0.15.1's open items for 0.16 (its sections 4, 5,
6 and 9) are folded in here and in section 14.

### What 1.21 does (read from the game files, 2026-11-24)
- **`merchant_government`** (`00_government_types.txt`): rules `landless_playable`, `profit_trade_routes = yes`,
  `distribution_trade_routes = no`, `can_have_vassal_suzerain = yes`, `can_sponsor_construction = yes`; domicile
  `merchant_company`; county-tier title; flags `cannot_be_vassal_or_liege`, `government_is_merchant`,
  `government_has_merchant_company`. Modifiers: **`men_at_arms_cap = -99`** (no men-at-arms at all), mercenaries
  +100%, travel speed +20%, prestige gain -50%
- **Ours, before 0.16** (`jip_familial_government_types.txt`): MaA allowed (upkeep +50%), mercenaries -25%, income +15%,
  diplomatic range x2, legitimacy, noble families, `council = no`, estate domicile, duchy-tier title, vassal
  contract `mishpacha_vassal`
- **The patron**: the group `tributary_merchant` (`subject_contract_groups.txt`) is `is_tributary` and
  `is_beneficiary_relationship`, can't be broken from below, passes to both heirs, and its contracts are
  `merchant_taxes` and `merchant_prestige` (plus two shown only to merchants). It is valid only for
  `is_landless_merchant = yes` with a landed suzerain, so **families can't use it as it is**. The patron is
  whoever holds the company's county, set by script (`start_tributary_interaction_effect`); moving the company
  swaps patron (`gdp_move_company_departure_effect`). Nothing in it checks a DLC
- **Become Merchant** (`become_merchant_decision`, `gdp_merchant_decisions.txt`): `has_gdp_dlc_trigger`; for
  adventurers, republics, landed counts and dukes; the conversion runs in the event `gdp_merchant.0001`, not
  one scripted effect; 5 years of `gdp_voluntary_merchant_modifier`; all prestige lost
- **Merchants can be evicted**: vanilla's eviction picks a new patron outside the expelling realm, an overlap
  with our expulsion
- **The council tab** (`hud.gui`, `tab_council`) is hidden by a hard check, `Not( Or( IsLandlessAdventurer(
  GetPlayer ), IsLandlessMerchant( GetPlayer ) ) )`: no flag or rule reaches it. The trade tab shows for
  `GetGovernment.HasRule( 'profit_trade_routes' )` or a landed ruler
- **Custom landless starts are bookmark data, not GUI**: a bookmark character takes `character_design_type =
  landless_adventurer | noble_family | merchant` and a `government` (`00_bookmarks.txt`: the custom adventurer
  of 867, the Chinese noble family of 1066, the Silk Road merchant of 1178), and the bookmark screen shows it as
  a "new" character opening the ruler designer (`StartDesignLandlessNobleFamily`, `IsDesigningLandless`)

**Tested in game by Stav, 2026-11-25** (F1-F8, PR #58): everything works.

  be-Av, a second Purim
