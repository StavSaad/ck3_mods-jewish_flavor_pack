# Rising Lion v0.15.0 — Beth Aramaye: the Arameans and the Gnostics of the East

Started: 2026-10-24  
Status (2026-11-02): all thirteen sections built, in testing. Left: the tests still open (Testing, below)

The ancient Levant and Mesopotamia through the peoples who still spoke Aramaic in 867: the Arameans, the
Assyrians, the Sabians of Harran and the Mandaeans of the marshes, and the old gods they could bring back.

---

## What vanilla already covers (don't duplicate)

- **Church of the East**: `faith:nestorian`, head of faith `d_patriarchate_in_the_east` (landless, Baghdad,
  full Catholicos line), Order of Saint Addai, Nestorian branch of Beth Nahrain
- **Syriac Orthodox**: `antiochian_rite` of Miaphysitism (Holy Myron, loyalty to Muslim rulers); the
  ecclesiastical government, clerical regions and councils of the 1.20 Christian content
- **Beth Nahrain** (vanilla, extended in `common/decisions/81_major_decisions_middle_east.txt`): Assyrian
  empire over `k_jazira` + `k_mesopotamia`; promotes Syriac-heritage cultures in `custom_beth_nahrain`
- **Mandaeans and Sabians**: one faith, `mandaean_faith` (dualism religion), with the rites `mandeaism`
  (pacifism, esotericism, Christian syncretism) and `sabianism` (Islamic syncretism, astrology, esotericism).
  But:
  - no county, character or dynasty has it at any start date: the faith is only ever reached by conversion
  - holy sites are borrowed: Shiraz (`farz`, a Nestorian site), Basra (eminent), Baghdad, Damascus, Jerusalem.
    Neither Harran nor the marshes (`c_tib`, `c_batiha`) are holy
  - the dualism religion has `doctrine_spiritual_head`, but the faith has no `religious_head`
  - hooks: Muslim *zandaqa* grouping (`faiths_are_shia_zandaqa_sub_group` and the two triggers next to it in
    `00_religious_triggers.txt`), alcohol taboo, god names
  - the Harranian star-worshippers (a pagan cult of Sin, Bel and Nabu) are folded into the Mandaeans as a rite

Decision taken: **keep vanilla's Sabian rite** under `mandaean_faith` and build on it; the old gods come back
through a separate pagan faith (below), not by moving the rite.

## 9. Feast override and variants

Our festivals are variants of vanilla's feast, the way the Mongol New Year is one. This version builds the
override with Akitu (section 7) and the Mandaean and Harranian feasts; the Jewish feasts (Pesach, Purim,
Sukkot, Hanukkah) reuse it in 0.16 ([v0.16_exilarchs_and_maccabees.md](v0.16_exilarchs_and_maccabees.md)).

### How vanilla does it (the model)
- `activity_feast` (`common/activities/activity_types/feast.txt`) has an option category `special_type`
  (`special_option_category = special_type`) with four variants: `feast_type_generic`,
  `feast_type_legendary`, `feast_type_murder`, `feast_type_tsagaan_sar`
- **Tsagaan Sar** (Khans of the Steppe): `is_shown` nomads of Mongolic or Turkic heritage; `is_valid` months
  1-3 and not `recent_tsagaan_sar_feast_var`; 2.5x a feast's cost; `is_tsagaan_sar` checks the option; hooks
  in the meal and toast phases start its chain; its pulse events are in `feast_default_event_selection`
- The toast phase's summary (`feast.7201`) only fires for the generic feast, so a variant brings its own

## 10. Sabian storyline: the Return of the Moon God

Playing the Sabians from a protected minority to the restorers of the old gods and their lands. It ties
sections 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 into one arc.

### Where it starts (vanilla 867)
- `c_harran` is held by Hassan of the Banu Shayban (`levantine000004`, Muslim), a direct vassal of
  `e_arabia`; with section 2 the county itself is Sabian
- The caliphate is in the Anarchy at Samarra (861–870); the Zanj revolt (vanilla's
  `fp3_story_cycle_zanj_rebellion`) breaks out in the Basra marshes in 869, Mandaean country. Both are
  openings for a minority to rise
- Vanilla models to borrow from: `ep3_story_cycle_hasan_sabbah` and `ep3_story_cycle_restoring_rome`
  (a long goal carried by a story cycle), `book_translation_story_cycle` (scholarship, fits Thabit)

### Structure
- [x] **Start** (decided): **Thabit ibn Qurra's house in Baghdad** only, a playable Familial family at 867
  (section 8). No fictional Harranian head; the road to Harran is part of the story
- [x] **Story cycle** `story_jip_sabian_revival` (`common/story_cycles/jip_story_sabian_revival.txt`, shown in the story
  window like Hasan's: the act, the Sabians' standing 0-10, the patron, the initiates and the act's decisions; decision
  group `jip_story_sabian_revival`): started before the lobby for the holder of `d_jip_harrani`
  (`jip_sabian_story_on_actions.txt`; Thabit starts as a secret Mesopotamian pagan, the first initiate, and a player gets
  `jip_sabian_story.1001` as the game begins), passed to the primary heir if a Sabian (or old-faith) believer, and to the new head
  when one leaves the faith (`jip_mishpacha_leave_faith_effect`); otherwise it ends
- [x] **A chain of decisions** the player can see, each unlocking the next (like the Hasan Sabbah chain), with
  milestone events from the story cycle: the story's own decision group, listed in the story window, each act's
  decisions opening with it

### Acts
1. **The Sabians of Baghdad** (Familial family, section 8). Done (stage 1): standing (`jip_sabian_story_raise/lower_standing_effect`)
   rises with translation, horoscope and physician contracts for Muslim patrons (`jip_sabian_story_contract_effect` in
   `jip_contract_finish_effect`), two or more Sabian Court Astrologers at Muslim courts (every two years), the Question's
   defence (+2, failure -2) and events `jip_sabian_story.1010`-`1050` (`events/jip_sabian_story_events.txt`); at 10 the act
   ends (`.1090`). Keep the Mysteries (`jip_sabian_keep_the_mysteries_decision`, `.1100`) makes the head and the house's
   adults at court secret Mesopotamian pagans (the story's `jip_sabian_initiates`); an initiate found out by a Muslim
   brings `.1040`. (Revealing the old gods changes a Familial head's religion, which `jip_family_left_faith_on_action`
   treats as leaving the family's faith: the unveiling updates the families' recorded religion, so they keep their titles)
   - defend the community's status in the "People of the Book" challenge (section 2)
   - win standing at the caliph's court: translation, astronomy, horoscopes (contracts; the book translation
     story)
   - keep the old rites in secret: family members may hold the Mesopotamian pagan faith as a vanilla
     **secret faith** (the *Fihrist*'s Harranian mysteries)
2. **Return to Harran**: move the estate to Harran and become its count, by one of three roads. Done (stage 2):
   `jip_sabian_petition_for_harran_decision` (`.2010`-`.2012`), `jip_sabian_buy_harran_decision` (`.2020`-`.2022`) and
   `jip_sabian_raise_the_harranians_decision` (`.2030`; vanilla's `claim_cb`, started by script, with a spawned army: a
   landless head can fight, as vanilla's adventurers do); gaining Harran by any means ends the act (`.2090`). An owner who
   holds Harran in Act I skips to Act III too, and one who holds it as an independent ruler in Act I or II straight to Act IV
   (`.3090`; `jip_sabian_story_harran_held_effect`)
   - **appointment**: petition the liege for the governorship (a Shtadlanut-style request with an AI
     acceptance value)
   - **purchase**: buy the tax farm, at a high gold cost
   - **seizure**: during a crisis (the Anarchy, a liege at war, a fragmenting caliphate) raise the Harranians
     in revolt for the county
   - on gaining Harran the family takes its liege's government, as Mishpacha families do
3. **Throw off the caliph**: independence, by war or by riding a fragmenting caliphate. Done (stage 3):
   `jip_sabian_proclaim_freedom_decision` asks the liege (`.3010`), who may let Harran go (likelier while at war); a refusal
   can be answered with vanilla's `independence_war` and a Harranian army (`.3011`). Independence won any way ends the act
   (`.3090`); losing Harran sends the story back to Act II. Required, because only
   an independent Sabian can restore the old gods (decided)
4. **Restore the Old Gods** (section 4). Done (stage 4): the mysteries are unveiled (`jip_sabian_unveil_the_mysteries_effect`)
   when the story's owner finishes the Temple of Sin (`jip_sabian.0001`), or through
   `jip_sabian_unveil_the_mysteries_decision` for an owner who comes to a temple already standing:
   - the owner proclaims the old gods if they haven't; the initiates drop their secret and take the owner's rite, and the
     list is cleared, so the story window stops showing it (the patron is gone from Act IV too)
   - every Sabian character and county in the world turns to Sinism, and Sabianism is closed to converts; Sabian families
     keep their titles (their recorded religion follows) and their features (`jip_is_sabian_rite_trigger`: Sabianism or
     the old faith)
   - Muslim neighbours' opinion and fervor; Mandaean rulers disown the owner (section 3)
   - `.4010` names a High Priest of Sin as court chaplain (not a theocratic vassal: Harran has no temple holding to give);
     the next Akitu or Feast of Nisan ends the act (`.4090`)

   The original design:
   - the secret believers reveal themselves together; Sabian counties convert to the new faith quickly
   - the Temple of Sin at Harran is rebuilt; the first Akitu (section 7)
   - a high priest of Sin as a theocratic vassal (vanilla's ecclesiastical government)
   - the Muslim world reacts: open paganism at Harran draws holy wars from Muslim neighbours
5. **Reclaim the land**. Done (stage 5): `jip_sabian_claim_cult_city_decision` (a pressed claim, then vanilla's claim war;
   not a new casus belli), `jip_sabian_bring_the_gods_home_decision` (`.5010`-`.5012`: the statues of Sin, Ishtar, Ashur,
   Bel, Shamash and Marduk, the six restored temples; Babylon is a cult city to claim, `b_al-hila` in `c_kufa`, vanilla's
   Babylon holy site), and the ending `.5090`
   - a casus belli for the counties of the old cult centres (the holy sites of section 4 and the lands of the
     section 5 kingdoms)
   - **bring the gods home**: the cult statues carried off long ago come back as artifacts, each installed in a
     restored temple (Sin at Harran, Ishtar at Arbela, Ashur at Ashur/Nineveh, Nabu, Marduk); each temple
     speeds conversion and lets Akitu be held there
   - the empires of section 5 by rite: the Palmyrene Empire (Aramean) or Beth Nahrain (Assyrian); Aram-Damascus,
     Osroene, Adiabene and Hatra are names and CoAs on existing titles

## Decided

- Keep vanilla's Sabian rite under `mandaean_faith`; the old gods return through a separate pagan faith
