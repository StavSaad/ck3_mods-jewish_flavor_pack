# Rising Lion v0.15.1 — the Florin update

Started: 2026-11-15  
Status: planning; nothing can be built before the patch is out (17 November 2026)

A compatibility release for CK3 1.21 "Florin", the free update that ships with the Silk & Silver expansion.
No new content of ours: 0.15's features working on the new game, and the few places where the new systems
replace something we built by hand. New features that use trade and merchants go to 0.16 or later
(section 9).

**What is known** (from the dev diaries and the store page, all work in progress; check everything against
the patch's files and notes on the day):

| Change | Free or paid | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Every regular building removed; counties get **district** slots (military, market, urban, rural…) with cheaper buildings inside; special buildings mostly stay, with new effects | free | DD #1 |
| **Settlement Size** 1-10 per barony sets the number of district slots | free | DD #1 |
| **Control** drifts to a baseline; **Popular Opinion** becomes a bar from -100 to +100 | free | DD #1 |
| Every cost and gold income reviewed; broad modifiers may be split (e.g. "maritime income") | free | DD #1 |
| Trade markets (about 900), resource districts for 40+ goods, buildings with needs, tolls, roads that decay, reworked traditions | not stated | DD #2, #8 |

Sources: ck3db.com/dlc/silk-and-silver; Paradox forum dev diaries "Silk & Silver" #1-#8 and Dev Diary #203
(Adventurer Changes); Paradox's preview of the modding changes (2026-11-17), whose renames are folded into
sections 1-4 below with the mod's own uses counted (comments and the generated copies left out).

**From the modding preview**, what touches us:
- Holdings are districts with buildings inside; a special building needs a small district around it
  (`type = special`); great districts (`type = great_district`, with `rebuild_cost` and `can_rebuild`) and their
  slots are set per province. Building effects run in **district** scope. A building's name is `<key>`, its
  description `<key>_desc` (no `building_` prefix)
- Development is **prosperity**; county opinion is **popular opinion**; control drifts to a baseline
  (`monthly_county_control_growth/decline` removed); tax is split into eleven categories
- Trade is data: goods, categories (`need = yes`), price and production modifiers per good and category,
  `trade_good_need` on buildings, one market per de jure duchy at its `market_capital` barony
- New: region `province_modifier`/`county_modifier`, `not_parameter` in culture and rite modifiers, decision
  pickers for a building, market, route or good category, trade on_actions

**From the preview changelog** (forum, 2026-11-17; "not final"), what touches us, folded into the sections:
- **Great projects**: every great project building now lives in a **Great Project District slot, which every
  province has**; special buildings each get their own one-building special district, "reworked for the new
  economy, with needs and often settlement size bonuses". The Cathedral great project (our model) is one chain
  now; great projects' minimum gold costs are lower
- **Holy sites**: their development bonuses were *not* carried over to prosperity (vanilla dropped them)
- **New vanilla cultures in our lands** (they replace ours: section 7): **Mandaean** (Syriac heritage) at Tib,
  Batiha (the Marshes) and Rusafa; **Remenkhemi** (Egyptian, from Ancient Egyptian) in 14 counties along the Nile; **Musta'arabi** (Israelite
  heritage, Arabic language) for 83 historical characters and merchant dynasties
- **Merchants exist without the DLC** (AI companies, Jewish Musta'arabi and Radhanite ones among them, and "the Ben
  Netira bankers of Baghdad" in their home county). A merchant's patron is whoever holds its holding: **merchants
  can be tributaries of vassals**, the very tie our families can't have
- **Adventurers**: most of it is base game (contracts, events, story cycles, decisions, camp officers);
  Freebooters and Legitimists stay Roads to Power. Estates aren't mentioned
- **Religion**: a new Precepts category: every faith has Dietary Restrictions, Alcohol and **Usury** doctrines
  (Accepted, Discouraged, Outsiders Only, Forbidden); new Judaism doctrines **Rabbinic Judaism** (competitiveness)
  and **Toraic Judaism** (trade capacity); almost every tenet changed
- **Economy**: control in levels -10..+10 toward a baseline, popular opinion -4..+4 toward a baseline; every
  men-at-arms type has a **control stat**, costs less to raise and more to keep in peace; estate buildings give
  less and cost more; artifacts give no tax; the Persian Struggle no longer takes the Caliph's vassals of other
  faiths as Supporters, nor lets adventurers end it by passing through
- **Script**: `monthly_income` (characters) vs `monthly_tax` (provinces); `market_capital` is an optional
  override on duchies; province history restructured (`province_mapping` removed; holding districts, district
  buildings and province size settable in history); domicile types take `initial_buildings`; `start_war`
  validates its casus belli; a scripted rule for who can be a Trader
