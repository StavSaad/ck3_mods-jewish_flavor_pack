# Rising Lion v0.15.1 — the Florin update

Started: 2026-11-15  
Status: planning; nothing can be built before the patch is out (17 November 2026)

A compatibility release for CK3 1.21 "Florin", the free update that ships with the Silk & Silver expansion.
No new content of ours: 0.15's features working on the new game, and the few places where the new systems
replace something we built by hand. New features that use trade and merchants go to 0.16 or later
(section 9).

**What is known** (from the dev diaries and the store page, all work in progress; check everything against
the patch's files and notes on the day):

| Change | Free or paid | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Every regular building removed; counties get **district** slots (military, market, urban, rural…) with cheaper buildings inside; special buildings mostly stay, with new effects | free | DD #1 |
| **Settlement Size** 1-10 per barony sets the number of district slots | free | DD #1 |
| **Control** drifts to a baseline; **Popular Opinion** becomes a bar from -100 to +100 | free | DD #1 |
| Every cost and gold income reviewed; broad modifiers may be split (e.g. "maritime income") | free | DD #1 |
| Trade markets (about 900), resource districts for 40+ goods, buildings with needs, tolls, roads that decay, reworked traditions | not stated | DD #2, #8 |
| **Landless adventurers** move into the base game; "some of the more specialised purposes and contracts" stay in Roads to Power; the line isn't drawn yet | free | DD #203 |
| **Merchants**: a landless government, a family trading company in a city domicile under a Patron (the county's holder) | paid | DD #3 |
| **Republics** playable (one government, Administration types, Assembly, patricians, doge), Trade Posts, Trade Leagues, Guelphs & Ghibellines | paid | store page |
| Bookmarks 1066 Channels of Commerce and 1178 Guelphs & Ghibellines | paid | store page |

Sources: ck3db.com/dlc/silk-and-silver; Paradox forum dev diaries "Silk & Silver" #1-#8 and Dev Diary #203
(Adventurer Changes).

## 2. Buildings and districts

The mod defines no regular building, only 24 special ones (`common/buildings/01_jip_buildings.txt`), and
special buildings "mostly stay, with new effects". So the work is format and balance, not redesign.

- [ ] **The building format**: read 1.21's `_buildings.info` and one converted vanilla special building
  (the Tomb of Cyrus, a holy site temple, Hadrian's Wall for Masada). Convert all 24: any renamed or
  removed fields, how a special building sits beside districts (its own slot, or a district of its own),
  and whether `asset` blocks and our ziggurat models still attach the same way
- [ ] **Special slots in history**: `zz_jip_province_changes.txt` sets 18 `special_building_slot` and one
  `special_building`. Check the keys still exist in province history, and that vanilla hasn't taken any of
  our baronies for a slot of its own or a resource district (Harran, Ashur, Arbela, Hatra, Babylon,
  Palmyra, Manbij, Emesa, Dura, Tyre, Sidon, Byblos, Baalbek, Kition, Tunis, Masada's `b_zughar`,
  Jerusalem)
- [ ] **Settlement Size**: if it lives in province history, give our cult cities and Jerusalem sizes that
  fit 867 (Harran a living city, Ashur and Hatra ruins of size 1, Babylon a village beside Hilla) wherever
  vanilla's are wrong for them; override by key or province, never the whole file
- [ ] **Script effects**: `add_special_building`, `add_special_building_slot`, `remove_building`,
  `has_building`, `has_building_or_higher` are used throughout the temples, the Temple Mount, Damascus's
  mosque and the story's scripted GUIs. Confirm each still exists under its name (the effect docs are in
  `ck3.exe`; memory)
- [ ] **The holy site fallback slot effect**: vanilla's version will change with the rework (holy site
  buildings may become districts or keep their slot). Rebuild our override on 1.21's, keeping the Ziggurat
  and the classical temple for Mesopotamian and Aramean sites, and Nineveh's Lalish check
- [ ] **Vanilla regular buildings named in our script**: `paddy_fields_01`, `plantations_01`,
  `orchards_01`, `farm_estates_01`, `cereal_fields_01`, `hill_farms_01`, `longhouses_01` (in the copied
  vanilla files; they regenerate) — after regenerating, grep for any left in our own script
- [ ] **The temples' modifiers**: every income and cost is reviewed, so rebalance against 1.21's special
  buildings of the same rank: Masada's three tiers (garrison, forts, levy), the Temple in Jerusalem, the
  Cleared Mount, the sixteen temples of the old gods. Use the new split modifiers where they fit (a
  Phoenician temple at a port: maritime income, if that exists)
- [ ] **Great projects**: `jip_great_projects.txt` and `jip_third_temple_projects.txt` place special
  buildings on completion; costs are in the review. Diff vanilla's `pam_cathedral_01` and rescale ours by
  the same ratio
- [ ] **Buildings with needs** (if free): decide per temple whether it has a need at all. Recommended:
  none in 0.15.1 (a temple that runs at half strength for lack of incense is a 0.16 idea, section 9)
- [ ] **Estate buildings** are domicile buildings, a different system; expected untouched, but check costs
  against vanilla's reviewed estate lines, since ours were copied from them
- [ ] **Model check in game**: the three ziggurats, the generic one, the Mediterranean temples and Hadad's
  mosque model still appear, on the right locator, facing south-west, with districts drawn around them

## 3. Development, control and popular opinion

- [ ] **Modifier names**: 33 `development_growth_factor`, 4 `development_growth`, 12
  `monthly_county_control_growth_*`, 10 `county_opinion_add` and 2 `county_opinion` uses, in the buildings,
  two court position tasks, the perks, the character and county modifiers, an on_action, a holy site and
  the feast events. If control or popular opinion change shape (baseline and drift), some of these
  modifiers will be renamed or mean something else: list 1.21's from the modifier docs and map each
- [ ] **Scale**: popular opinion from -100 to +100. A flat `county_opinion_add = 10` may now be a tenth of
  what it was, or the same: compare vanilla's values for the same kind of source and rescale
- [ ] **Direct changes**: one `change_development_progress` and four `development_level` checks (the Nabataean
  Agriculture's capital development; the family spawn's weighting by development; the Harran price).
  Confirm the effect and the value's range, and that `jip_sabian_harran_price_value` still gives a sane
  price if development numbers are lower overall
- [ ] **The dynasty perks**: Clever Administrators (+50% development growth, +1 domain limit, +20% tax) and
  Return of the Gods (development growth, county opinion) against 1.21's vanilla legacies; the economy is
  being slowed, so +50% and +20% are likely far above the new norm
- [ ] **Family spawn quotas** (`01_jip_jewish_family_spawn_effects.txt`): weighted by development and royal
  seats. If Settlement Size is readable from script, weight by it as well or instead: it says "a city"
  better than development does. Only if it is a one-line change; else 0.16
- [ ] **Expulsion and migration** use county opinion and control in places
  (`01_jip_expulsion_effects.txt`, `01_jip_migration_effects.txt`): reread both with the new meanings

## 5. Adventurers in the base game

The largest opportunity in the patch: much of the family mechanics is "Roads to Power only" today.

- [ ] **Read where the line is drawn**: which of these are free in 1.21: the landless playable character,
  the camp domicile, `landless_adventurer_government`, estates (administrative, almost certainly still
  paid), **task contracts** (the system, as opposed to particular contracts), travel and provisions,
  `has_dlc_feature = roads_to_power` and vanilla's `has_ep3_dlc_trigger` (does the trigger still mean
  what our gates need?)
- [ ] **Our gates**, twenty files: the family spawn at game start (`jip_jewish_families_game_start_on_action`,
  Roads to Power only), `jip_mishpacha_contract_owner_trigger`, the estate buildings and their vanilla
  overrides, the Jewish and gnostic court positions and tasks, the expulsion effects (expelled lords
  becoming families), the domicile on_actions, provisions values, three bookmark portraits
- [ ] **The question that decides it**: families live in an **estate**, the administrative domicile. If
  estates stay paid, the families stay Roads to Power only and 0.15.1 only confirms the gates still work.
  If a domicile type becomes free (the camp) the families could run without the DLC only by changing
  domicile type: a 0.16 decision, not a compatibility fix
- [ ] **What opens at once if contracts are free**: nothing for the families without the estate. Keep the
  gates as they are; loosen them only where the patch plainly allows it and a no-DLC test passes
- [ ] **Test without the DLCs**: launch with Roads to Power off, and with Silk & Silver off: no family
  titles left ownerless, the bookmark's Exilarchs and Pinchas still valid or cleanly hidden, no errors
  from estate script
- [ ] **Vanilla's cleanup of landless titles** (`yearly_on_actions.txt`, section 1) will have been
  rewritten for free adventurers: make sure the family titles of tributary heads still survive it
- [ ] **Scripted rules** (`jip_scripted_rules.txt`: `can_move_domicile` and the war check): rebuild on
  1.21's version; adventurers' and Merchants' rules will have changed around ours
- [ ] **Eldad ha-Dani** (0.16 section 5) no longer needs `dlc_feature = roads_to_power` on his title if
  the adventurer government is free: note it there

## 6. Merchants and Republics beside the families

The expansion's Merchant is a landless family with a domicile in a city under a Patron: the Familial
government's own shape, arriving in vanilla. 0.15.1 keeps the two apart and working; whether they should
meet is 0.16's question (section 9).

- [ ] **No collision**: the Merchant government's flags, `mechanic_type` and domicile type against ours
  (`mechanic_type = familial`, `domicile_type = estate`, `government_uses_domicile_but_not_adventurer`).
  Vanilla triggers like "is a landless ruler with a domicile" may now sweep the families into merchant
  content (company events, trade routes, the Patron tie): play a family for ten years with the DLC on and
  read every event that isn't ours
- [ ] **Two families in one city**: a Merchant company and a Mishpacha estate in the same county. Check the
  estate can still settle (`can_move_domicile`), the family's liege tie (vassal or tributary of the
  realm) beside the company's Patron tie, and the family spawn's one-per-county rule
- [ ] **City holdings**: families hold only cities and adopt the liege's government on gaining land. If
  cities now belong to republics' and merchants' systems (patricians, Trade Posts), check
  `jip_mishpacha_*` land handling and the charter's trade rights still make sense
- [ ] **Reestablish Canaanite City** makes a Phoenician republic vassal in Tyre, Sidon, Beirut, Tripoli,
  Famagusta, Nicosia or Latakia. Republics are rebuilt: the government key, how a republic vassal is
  created, whether it needs an Administration type and an Assembly set up by script. Rebuild the effect on
  vanilla's own "create a republic" script; with the DLC these become playable
- [ ] **The Radhanite start** (867, tribal, Tmutarakan) and the Radhanite culture: unchanged in 0.15.1.
  Check its traditions against the reworked ones
- [ ] **Realm frames**: `gui/shared/coat_of_arms.gui` gains merchant and republic frames; our Familial
  frame, kippah, Keter, crescent and klila must still be chosen for our government and not theirs
- [ ] **New bookmarks**: 1066 Channels of Commerce and 1178 Guelphs & Ghibellines. Our 867 bookmark is
  untouched; check that title history (the Exilarchate at 1066 and 1178, the family titles) has valid
  holders at both dates and that the family spawn runs there

## 9. Not in 0.15.1: ideas the patch opens

Written down so they aren't lost; each belongs in the 0.16 plan once the patch can be read.

- **Radhanites as Merchants**: the Radhanite start as a Merchant company (Caravaneer) with the DLC, the
  tribal start kept without it
- **Families and companies**: a Jewish Merchant company keeping the family's Jewish layer (Mar, the kippah,
  the Beit Knesset, Shtadlanut), or a family that founds a company. Needs the Merchant government's
  script in hand
- **Phoenician Trade League**: the restored Canaanite cities as a league; Tyre's purple and the cedars as
  goods the temples of Melqart and the Temple's cedar contribution (0.16 section 7) ask for
- **Temples with needs**: incense, oil and grain for the temples of the old gods and the Temple in
  Jerusalem; a temple without its offerings gives less piety
- **Families without Roads to Power**, if a free domicile allows it
- **The half-shekel** (0.16 section 7) as a trade-like flow, if routes can carry gold between rulers
